# FM Blackthorn original font

Starting commit: `4aa5726c78f56ae3742e3240d10d1897e92df5d5` on the clean reviewed branch. Isolated branch: `codex/fm-font12-original` in `/private/tmp/freemotion-font12-20261004`.

This adds a twelfth distinct original FreeMotion family: upright modern blackletter with broad-nib diamond terminals, broken minuscule arches, angular bowls and true ascenders/descenders. Regular and Bold WOFF2 faces each map 234 characters, including all printable ASCII and common accented Latin. The generator draws every glyph from hand-placed geometry without reading a third-party font, outline, image or metrics file. Its dark, upright minuscule rhythm differs from Circuit Sans's lighter condensed angular forms, Aperture Stencil's open bridges, Palais Deco's engraved small caps and Ribbon Script's slanted joins.

An independent strict critic held the first specimen because B resembled 8 and 6 resembled lowercase a. B was redrawn with a continuous left spine and two separate open bowls; 6 now has an exposed upper hook feeding one lower loop. The regenerated specimen includes native 32 px and 48 px text plus an explicit 32 px B/8 and 6/a comparison. Blind re-review accepted the revised design for titles and captions, with those pairs distinct at 32 px. Use good contrast and roughly 32 px or larger; this display face is not designed for dense body text or tiny labels. The broader request for more distinct original fonts remains open.
