# C31 — video Time Warp Scan cold seeks

Starting commit: `51a2043af7cf8eb7e2781c6202f5e6b57ae36896` on isolated `codex/690-c31-timewarp-video`. Exact standing brief: `REQUESTS.md` #690 and the C31 row in the idle-backlog plan; no matching `audits/*.json` entry.

A straight, unparented, uncropped video with only Time Warp Scan now rebuilds its historical Freeze or Reveal picture after a jump. A separate video element decodes the crossing times and draws only each crossed strip into an output-sized accumulator. It keeps one committed accumulator and one temporary replacement during preparation, never a list of decoded frames. Sequential export copies the previous accumulator and adds just the new strip; an older preview request is cancelled when a newer seek arrives. The paused preview first paints provisionally, then requests a repaint once its current scan is ready. MP4, GIF and PNG-sequence main renderers await the prepared picture. Worker export explicitly falls back to main for video Time Warp Scan until transferred-plate parity is proved. A new MP4 resume token prevents mixing old and new pixels.

Changed files: `js/frames.js` (bounded cancellable decoder/accumulator), `js/compositor.js` (narrow eligibility, strip rendering and prepared plate), `js/app.js` (preview repaint), `js/exporter.js` and `js/export-worker.js` (await/fallback/resume), `index.html` (all changed-script cache tags), and one `tests/tests.js` regression tagged `TBD`.

The focused indexed-H.264 regression independently decodes all 30 fixture frames, then compares continuous playback with cold Freeze/Reveal at 128 px and 64 px, moving video, horizontal and looped scans. It checks superseded-job cancellation, Worker fallback, all MP4 frame indices against sequential playback, and the resume token. The prior still-image scan regression also passed. JavaScriptCore syntax and `git diff --check` passed. Several browser starts missed app scripts; the focused runner loaded those same local assets before testing, and both checks passed on the final run. This is a local fix, not a release.
