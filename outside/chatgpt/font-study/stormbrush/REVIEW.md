# FM Stormbrush proof — held for completion

An original drawn dry-brush display study, built from cubic stroke ribbons with independently varied width and ragged edges. No existing font outlines are read by the builder. The comparison image opens existing FreeMotion fonts only to render side-by-side specimens.

The 26 separately drawn capitals, 26 separately drawn lowercase, 10 separately drawn figures and all printable ASCII punctuation exist in Regular and Bold. Common Latin accents, Æ/æ, Œ/œ, ß and 22 symbols/typographic marks extend each proof to 181 mapped characters. The 24/32/48/64/96 px specimen, 34 px upper/lower/figure/punctuation/accent/symbol rows and 32 px mixed-case sample are legible; the face is visibly more irregular and forceful than Lilt Marker, Blackthorn or Reed at the same displayed size. The first 9 read like a lowercase a and was redrawn to distinguish it from 0/6. The thinner Regular at 24–34 px needs further contrast review, especially I/J, and the brush texture must stay clear in actual video frames. Æ/æ and Þ/þ also need closer live-size scrutiny: the ligatures are dense and Thorn can read like P/p.

The two `motion-frame-*.png` captures use the bundled sample video at confirmed advancing times (0.90s and 1.96s), with the proof WOFF2 overlaid in a browser at 96/38 px. The title and accented subtitle stay clear against both frames. This checks visual quality over moving footage; it is not a native FreeMotion compositor/export test.

**Held.** Do not wire the proof into the picker, count it as a finished original family, or release it until the weak glyphs/spacing are reviewed in the actual editor picker and an export-path check passes. If the completed set reads as a generic brush font, reject it instead of filling the catalogue.

Evidence: `specimen.png`, `comparison.png`, `motion-frame-1.png`, `motion-frame-2.png`, `motion-preview.html`, Regular/Bold TTF/WOFF2 proofs and `build_stormbrush.py`.
