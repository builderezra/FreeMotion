# FM Stormbrush proof — held for completion

An original drawn dry-brush display study, built from cubic stroke ribbons with independently varied width and ragged edges. No existing font outlines are read by the builder. The comparison image opens existing FreeMotion fonts only to render side-by-side specimens.

The 26 separately drawn capitals, 26 separately drawn lowercase, 10 separately drawn figures and all printable ASCII punctuation exist in Regular and Bold. Common Latin accents, Æ/æ, Œ/œ, ß and 22 symbols/typographic marks extend each proof to 181 mapped characters. The 24/32/48/64/96 px specimen, 34 px upper/lower/figure/punctuation/accent/symbol rows and 32 px mixed-case sample are legible; the face is visibly more irregular and forceful than Lilt Marker, Blackthorn or Reed at the same displayed size. The first 9 read like a lowercase a and was redrawn to distinguish it from 0/6. The thinner Regular at 24–34 px needs further contrast review, especially I/J, and the brush texture must stay clear in actual video frames. Æ/æ and Þ/þ also need closer live-size scrutiny: the ligatures are dense and Thorn can read like P/p.

The two `motion-frame-*.png` captures use the bundled sample video at confirmed advancing times (0.90s and 1.96s), with the proof WOFF2 overlaid in a browser at 96/38 px. The title and accented subtitle stay clear against both frames. This checks visual quality over moving footage; it is not a native FreeMotion compositor/export test. The `native-picker.png` capture shows the actual editor card with both 400/700 faces loaded; its 24 px `Abc` sample is quieter than the 32–96 px title evidence but retains the irregular stroke. A focused native-browser regression covers both face loads, the live picker and export font source.

**Accepted for the isolated local candidate.** This is a short-title display font, strongest at 32 px and above. The rare Æ/æ forms are dense at 34 px and the 24 px picker card underplays the brush texture, so neither is a reason to present this as a general-purpose reading face. No push, deployment or release occurred. Reject or refine it if Ezra's visual review finds the family too familiar; it remains separate from the rejected Volt/Torchcut/Undertow studies.

Evidence: `specimen.png`, `comparison.png`, `motion-frame-1.png`, `motion-frame-2.png`, `motion-preview.html`, `native-picker.png`, Regular/Bold TTF/WOFF2 proofs and `build_stormbrush.py`.
