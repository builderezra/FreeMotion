# FM Stormbrush proof — held for completion

An original drawn dry-brush display study, built from cubic stroke ribbons with independently varied width and ragged edges. No existing font outlines are read by the builder. The comparison image opens existing FreeMotion fonts only to render side-by-side specimens.

The 26 separately drawn capitals, space, !, ? and . exist in Regular and Bold. The 24/32/48/64/96 px specimen and 34 px alphabet rows are legible; the face is visibly more irregular and forceful than Lilt Marker, Blackthorn or Reed at the same displayed size. The thinner Regular at 24–34 px needs further contrast review, especially I/J, and the brush texture must stay clear in actual video frames.

**Held.** Lowercase, figures, punctuation and Latin accents are not drawn. Do not wire the proof into the picker, count it as a finished original family, or release it until those are complete and the full set passes native-size picker/footage review. If the completed set reads as a generic brush font, reject it instead of filling the catalogue.

Evidence: `specimen.png`, `comparison.png`, Regular/Bold TTF proofs and `build_stormbrush.py`.
