# FM Reed — original family quality review

Starting product checkpoint: `d1ce0da9b8471ed3267735b92168abac9c79b1bf` on the clean Codex-only preferred branch. This isolated study draws a new high-contrast, curved alphabet grammar from variable-width paths: broad roots, swaying stems, pointed crowns, asymmetrical bowls and low floating bridges. The source reads no other font or outline. It is deliberately unlike the geometric, engraved, script and blackletter originals already in the product.

`build_reed.py` now draws all 26 capitals, a separately designed 26-letter lowercase, ten figures, every printable ASCII punctuation mark, the full Latin-1 supplement, and the common Œ/œ and € additions in Regular and Bold. `specimen.png` shows actual 24/32/48/64/96 px titles and complete 34 px alphabet, figure, punctuation, accent and symbol rows. `comparison.png` places the same mixed-case words beside FM Blackthorn, Ribbon Script and Palais Deco at 32 and 48 px; their binaries are read only for that comparison. Both generated TTFs are proof artifacts, not product font assets.

Visual verdict: **passes the local design gate for one family**. The title has a more individual rhythm than the rejected Volt, Torchcut and Undertow studies, and remains readable at 24 px. Lowercase is slender and distinct from reduced caps. The revised 6/9 separate from 0 at 34 px; punctuation and Latin accents match the drawn family. The ¼/½/¾ glyphs were redrawn after an overlapping first pass and now read at 34 px. N, G and the swept T were inspected again in 24/32 px full-word samples and remained recognizable. `native-picker.png` records the live picker sample and text layer. `motion-frame-1.png` and `motion-frame-2.png` show the title and accented subtitle over the repository's actually advancing sample video; the light lettering stays legible against both frames. The moving-video proof page is `motion-preview.html`. This supports the isolated candidate integration, while the broader request for many strong originals remains open.

The product source adds a small set of native kerning pairs and twelve typographic punctuation glyphs after those actual-size reviews, bringing the app faces to 206 mapped characters. The study TTFs stay here as the 194-character design proof; the app loads separately generated WOFF2 assets from `fonts/original/`. Do not revive the rejected font concepts or mistake this single family for completion of the many-font request.
