# FM Reed — title-letter proof (held)

Starting product checkpoint: `d1ce0da9b8471ed3267735b92168abac9c79b1bf` on the clean Codex-only preferred branch. This isolated study draws a new high-contrast, curved alphabet grammar from variable-width paths: broad roots, swaying stems, pointed crowns, asymmetrical bowls and low floating bridges. The source reads no other font or outline. It is deliberately unlike the geometric, engraved, script and blackletter originals already in the product.

`build_reed.py` now draws all 26 capitals, a separately designed 26-letter lowercase, ten figures and every printable ASCII punctuation mark in Regular and Bold. `specimen.png` shows actual 24/32/48/64/96 px titles and complete 34 px alphabet, figure and punctuation rows. `comparison.png` places the same mixed-case words beside FM Blackthorn, Ribbon Script and Palais Deco at 32 and 48 px; their binaries are read only for that comparison. Both generated TTFs are proof artifacts, not product font assets.

Visual verdict: **held, promising but incomplete**. The title has a more individual rhythm than the rejected Volt, Torchcut and Undertow studies, and remains readable at 24 px. Lowercase is slender and distinct from reduced caps. The revised 6/9 are distinct from 0 at 34 px; punctuation matches the drawn family. N and G received a second drawing pass and are more clearly named in the full-cap specimen, although their 24 px forms still deserve review. The T's swept crown is delicate. Latin accents remain absent. No font was wired into the picker, counted as delivered, or released.

Next: draw common Latin accents, refine spacing and kern pairs, and repeat actual-size, moving-footage and picker comparisons before product integration. Revisit N/G/T at 24–32 px during that gate.
