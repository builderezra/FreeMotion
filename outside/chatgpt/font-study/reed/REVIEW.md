Starting product checkpoint: `d1ce0da9b8471ed3267735b92168abac9c79b1bf` on the clean Codex-only preferred branch. This isolated study draws a new high-contrast, curved alphabet grammar from variable-width paths: broad roots, swaying stems, pointed crowns, asymmetrical bowls and low floating bridges. The source reads no other font or outline. It is deliberately unlike the geometric, engraved, script and blackletter originals already in the product.
