# FM Reed — title-letter proof (held)

Starting product checkpoint: `d1ce0da9b8471ed3267735b92168abac9c79b1bf` on the clean Codex-only preferred branch. This isolated study draws a new high-contrast, curved alphabet grammar from variable-width paths: broad roots, swaying stems, pointed crowns, asymmetrical bowls and low floating bridges. The source reads no other font or outline. It is deliberately unlike the geometric, engraved, script and blackletter originals already in the product.

`build_reed.py` currently draws ten capitals (A D E G H I N O R T), space, exclamation and question mark, in Regular and Bold. `specimen.png` shows the actual 24/32/48/64/96 px sizes. `comparison.png` places the same words beside FM Blackthorn, Ribbon Script and Palais Deco at 32 and 48 px; their binaries are read only for that comparison. Both generated TTFs are proof artifacts, not product font assets.

Visual verdict: **held, promising but incomplete**. The title has a more individual rhythm than the rejected Volt, Torchcut and Undertow studies, and remains readable at 24 px. The N's interlocking diagonal and G's opening still need a native-size legibility pass; the T's swept crown is delicate. The alphabet lacks sixteen capitals, all lowercase, numerals, most punctuation and accents. No font was wired into the picker, counted as delivered, or released.

Next: correct N/G/T at 24–32 px, draw the remaining capitals with the same organic construction, then develop a genuinely complementary lowercase rather than scaled capitals. Expand printable ASCII, common Latin accents and a second polished weight before considering product integration. Repeat actual-size, moving-footage and picker comparisons at the quality gate.
