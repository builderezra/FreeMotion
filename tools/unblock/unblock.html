Why I need you: that strip only exists on a web view bigger than its page, which is the installed app on a phone. #867 Controller & New group tiles

You were right about the colour, and the numbers agree: New group and Custom elements are 38 apart in RGB where this menu’s own rule says 60 minimum. At the opacity these tiles are painted at, that is no difference at all. They are also both dashed outlines, which is why they read as the same kind of thing.

Three options are drawn at the real PC tile size next to their actual neighbours, and again at phone size. Controller stays red in all three — “Null being red is good” is yours and I left it alone.

#869 Your projects have no backup

The code is on GitHub and can be put back to any release. Your projects are not. They live only in this browser’s storage on whichever device made them — clear site data, lose the phone, or delete one by mistake, and there is no undo. The only route today is exporting a file by hand, beforehand.

This is your data, and the sensible answers differ a lot, so I am not choosing it for you. The app is local-only by design and I am not changing that without you saying so.

#856 The Assistant’s paid keys

The talking AI is built and live in v16.23 — open a layer’s ⋯ menu, tap “Ask the Assistant”, and tell it what to change. That part is done and needs nothing from you.

What is left is the last line of what you asked for: “later when you pay for the pro version, it’ll unlock an API key for you to use like our own inbuilt but hidden API keys”. That one cannot be built the way it is described, and you should hear it from me. FreeMotion has no server — a key shipped inside the app is readable in the source and visible in the network call, so “hidden” is not possible. Hiding it needs a server of ours sitting between the app and the AI, and that would be the first thing in FreeMotion that stops being local-only.
