## Venetian Blinds (U1)
**Verdict:** Yes, the upgrade works and it is a clear improvement. Look Solid slats turns the cut strips into shaded slats in the slat colour with the picture between them, so a single clip no longer shows as black lines. Shut, it reads as a real blind, and Holes (still the default) is pixel-identical to today's main, so no saved project changes. Half open at the default Shading 45 % the slats look a bit flat and grey; Shading 70 % or a coloured slat such as walnut gives more depth (both are in the A/B).

- Invert makes the tilt shading run backwards (cosmetic, real). Look Solid slats + Invert On: the slats get darker as the blind CLOSES, and the kernel's own comment says they should get darker as it opens. The shading factor f = 1 - shade*(0.2 + 0.45*qk + 0.35*across) always uses qk, even when Invert makes the covered share 1-qk. Repro (?app=/ss/, dusk photo layer alone, 360 px, defaults otherwise): a fully shut blind with {look:1, invert:0, progress:0} has mean luma 188.1, and the same shut blind with {look:1, invert:1, progress:1} has 142.4. Thin 10 % slats: {look:1, invert:0, progress:0.9} gives slat red 135 (dark), {look:1, invert:1, progress:0.1} gives 206 (light). Wide 90 % slats: Off p0.1 gives 200-172, On p0.9 gives 168-138. Suggested fix: under flip, use (1-qk) in the tilt term.
- Opens from Middle + Solid slats puts a dark crease through every slat (cosmetic, design). across = |u-k-0.5|*2 equals 1 at the band boundary, which is the centre of each visible slat. So each slat goes light-dark-light across its width, not lit at one edge and shaded at the other the way Side mode looks. Repro: {look:1, opens:1} on any photo, progress 0.18-0.41 (row 3 of r5-blinds-bughunt.png).

## Starfield (U3)
**Verdict:** Yes, it works and it is a clear improvement. With Fly-through at 2× and Streak on, every star becomes a line pointing back at the focus, brightest at the star and fading toward the tail, which reads instantly as hyperspace; before, the same settings gave a sparse scatter of dots whose motion only shows in playback. Streak 0 gives exactly the same pixels as today's Starfield, but the look only holds up at Star size 3 and above, and frames get 5 to 11 times slower to render (bugs below).

- DOTTED STREAKS AT SMALL STAR SIZES (Star size 1 is the default). In the full-size render (1080 square, blank layer, t=1.0), streaks are a row of separate dots, not a line. Repro: ?app=/ss/, starfield {speed:2, size:1, amount:0.15, streak:100}. Crop x 6-16%, y 6-16% and zoom 4×: shots/r5-starfield-zoom4x.png. Size 2 also breaks into dots; size 4 stays continuous. Cause: starfieldMoving caps a streak at K = min(80, ceil(len / max(0.5, 0.6·th))) squares. At size 1 the squares are 0.7-1.4 px wide (th), so any streak longer than about 56-112 px (a star more than about 165-330 px from the focus) gets squares more than 2 px apart. The 360 px truth preview averages this away, so it shows only at full size and in export.
- STRIPED STREAKS AT SIZE 3 AND UP. Where the lines are continuous, streaks close to horizontal or vertical show a regular comb or ladder of stripes along their length. The upright squares are laid 0.6·th apart, so their overlapping coverage pulses. Repro: ?app=/ss/, starfield {speed:2, size:3, amount:0.3, streak:100}, t=1.0, rendered at 1080 px, crop x 8-18%, y 42-52% zoomed 4×: shots/r5-starfield-zoom4x-s3.png. Also plain to see at 600 px for size 8 on a 720 project: right-hand column of shots/r5-starfield-pair3.png.
- BIG SLOWDOWN. Each frame renders 5 to 11 times slower with Streak on, and the small preview is no cheaper because each star still makes up to 80 splat() calls. Times are for a whole 1080 square scene (blank layer, t=1.0, ms per frame, this laptop's Chrome): Fly-through 2× with size 1, density 0.5 goes from 19.9 to 103 ms at Streak 100; density 1 takes 230 ms; Streak 30 still takes 112 ms. Size 3, density 0.6 goes from 12.6 to 51 ms. In the 360 px preview, size 1, density 1 goes from 9.3 to 107 ms. At the default Star size this likely means only a few frames a second on a phone.
- MINOR, UI TEXT: Streak's note reads 'Each star becomes a line back toward the centre'. The lines actually point at Focus X/Y, which is not the centre once the focus is moved. Repro: {speed:2, size:3, amount:0.4, streak:100, x:0, y:100} streams from the corner.

## Lens Blur (U4)
**Verdict:** Yes, it works and it looks good: a clear improvement. On dusk and bush, today's strongest setting (Radius 30, Bloom 300%) is only a soft smear with no discs and an invisible Hexagon shape. At Radius 80-120 with bloom the lights open into clean round (or hexagonal) bokeh discs that look like a real lens. Defaults are unchanged, and the rack through 30-34 px is smooth (no jumps or black frames), but you have to raise Highlight bloom to get discs: at Bloom 0 small lights disappear at large radii.

- SLOWDOWN in a narrow band, 31-36 px. Repro: truth.html?app=/ss/, lensblur on dusk, 1080 project rendered at px 1080, {radius:R, bloom:300}, timing FM.renderScene (median of 5, this laptop). R30 290 ms, R31 592, R33 621, R34 532, R36 473, R37 209, R60 216, R90 128, R120 120 (no effect: 29). Cause: from 31 to 33 px both the 16-tap pass and lensBlurDense run (the blend), and from 34 to 36 px lensBlurDense runs at k=3 (about 450 taps for each small pixel). So that band costs about twice the old maximum and four to five times Radius 120. On a 1080x1920 export it is about 1 s of effect time per frame, the size the builder's own comment calls 'a trap on a phone'. A rack focus pays it on every frame that passes through.
- Cosmetic: the old 16-tap dotted spray shows through during the 30-34 px hand-over. Repro: ?app=/ss/, {radius:32, bloom:300} on dusk at px 1080, crop x 380-700, y 600-880 at 2x. Each bright light reads as a ring of separate dots laid over the new smooth disc, half-and-half at 32 px. This follows from keeping 30 px and under byte for byte, but a rack focus will flash it for a few frames.
- Cosmetic: the disc edges are slightly faceted at Radius 90-120 in a full-size render. Repro: ?app=/ss/, {radius:120, bloom:300} on dusk at px 1080, crop x 420-740, y 560-840 at 2x. The bilinear read-back from the k=8-10 grid leaves short straight segments with small bright beads every k px along each edge (a high-pass shows them up to about 9 levels). Not visible at screen size.

## Directional Blur (U5)
**Verdict:** Yes. Past 60 px the smear is truly solid, with no row of copies. It stays within 1 level on flat colour, Both ways is symmetric, and Behind, Ahead and Repeat all behave. 160 to 400 px gives a real whip-pan streak that the old 60 px limit could not reach, at about 10 ms extra per 1080 frame at 400 px. The weak spot is the old tap method it hands over from at 64 px and below, which posterizes colours, worst at high Quality.

- POSTERIZATION in the tap method (Distance 64 px and below; also in today's main, and still in the effects branch). Each tap is drawn 'lighter' at globalAlpha 1/N into an 8-bit canvas, so each tap's share is rounded to a whole level and a flat area comes out as N x round(v/N). Repro: truth.html?app=/ss/ (or /app/), a full-frame layer of flat grey bands, Frame edges Repeat, background black. At Distance 20 (the defaults, 9 taps): 3->0, 5->0, 6->9, 15->18, 31->27, 201->198, 255->252. An opaque layer also ends at alpha 252/255 (white over red reads 255,252,252), so 1.2% of whatever is underneath shows through. At Distance 60 with Quality 32: everything from 0 to 15 becomes 0, 99->96, 201->192, so only about 9 grey levels are left. On photos: a smooth gradient falls into 2 to 3 hue-shifted bands, the dusk sky gets a pink and mauve band, and the run clouds get blue, green and yellow blotches (shots/r5-motionblur-bug-banding.png; also visible in the third BEFORE row of r5-motionblur-ab). The dense method past 64 px is within 1 level. A fix direction: average the taps by pairwise halving as dirBlurDense does, or send high Quality through the dense method.
- FLICKER in the 60-64 px hand-over at high Quality (same cause as the bug above). The taps' weight (1-fD)/N changes with every pixel of Distance, so the posterized pattern changes every step. Repro: truth.html?app=/ss/, full-frame mclaren rendered at 540, Quality 32, Distance 59..65. Mean change per 1 px step is 0.3 (59->60), then 5.3, 6.1, 7.9, 6.7 (60->61->62->63->64), then 0.3 (64->65). On the run sky, coloured blotches appear at 60-63 px and vanish at 64 (shots/r5-motionblur-bug-handover.png). A keyframed Distance passing through 60-64 px will visibly flicker. At the default 9 taps the steps are 1.3 to 1.6, which is milder.
- MEMORY (found by reading the code, not measured). With Frame edges Repeat past 60 px, dirBlurDense calls edgePadPlate with margins of |dx|*D+2 and |dy|*D+2. The pooled _edgePad canvas then grows to, for example, 1650x2490 px (about 16 MB) for a 1080x1920 frame at 400 px and Angle 45; before the upgrade it was about 2.3 Mpx. FM.releaseTiltPools (compositor.js line 19220) now frees _dbP and _dbQ for that same iOS canvas-memory reason, but not _edgePad (lines 19551-19565). Only Directional Blur uses it, so the canvas stays held after an export or a project switch until a later Repeat call changes its size.

## Card Flip (U10)
**Verdict:** Yes, it works and is a clear improvement. With Flips Horizontal or Vertical the card now turns over to a different layer, the right way round. A layer with its eye off shows only on the card. There are no console errors or NaN frames, and a frame costs the same as Mirror (5-7 ms at 1080). Before, the far side could only be the same picture reversed, a flat colour or nothing. It looks good, but one real bug needs fixing first: if the back layer has its own 3D effect or tilt, the front face shows the wrong picture. Smaller flaws: faint mesh seams on the card while it holds turned over, and Flips: Both never shows the back.

- HIGH: the FRONT face shows the back layer's picture while it turns, if the back layer has its own mesh effect (Page Curl, Cube or another solid, a Card Flip with no Back layer) or a 3D tilt. Repro: truth.html?app=/ss/&extra=probe-fliplayer.js. At Flip 40 the front face shows CLIP 02's title card instead of the McLaren: mean change 39.6 (Page Curl) and 42.0 (tilt) against the same scene without them. Back: Mirror is unaffected (0.0). Cause: fliplayer runs tex=extractTex(A,bb) on the one shared _cfTex canvas BEFORE expand.other(p.source). Drawing the back layer then runs its own extractTex (pagecurl, solidFx, draw3DTiltLayer) and overwrites it. Fix: read the back plate first, or give the front its own canvas. Picture: shots/r5-fliplayer-clobber.png.
- MEDIUM: Flips: Both never shows the far side, so Back: Another layer does nothing there (nor do Solid colour or Transparent). Repro: compare __flip.scene({front:'front',back:'title',params:{mode:2,back:3,angle:120}}) with the same at back:2, and at back:1 with color '#00ff00'. Rendered, they are identical (diff 0.00) at Flip 60, 120, 150, 180 and 240. The card's normal keeps z = cos²(Flip) ≥ 0, so in Both mode it never turns over, although extractBackTex has a Both branch and the description says 'horizontally, vertically or both'. Picture: shots/r5-fliplayer-modes.png.
- MEDIUM: faint mesh seams over the back layer while the card is held turned over. Repro: back layer a flat #e7e2d5 image the card's size, eye off, Back 3, Flip exactly 180, rendered at 1080. 2.4% of the card's pixels are off by up to about 30 levels per channel, along the 8x8 triangle grid and its diagonals. - LOW-MEDIUM: a back layer bigger than the card (e.g. a full-frame photo) makes the turned card grow and shift. Repro: probe scene with the back layer's media set to a 1080x1080 full-frame image, Back 3, Flip 180, rendered at 720 px. Cause: fliplayer is not in SOLID_FX, so its bounding box is the fast alpha box with about 12 plate px of slack, and the back is cropped at that box. Fix: refine it with alphaBBoxWithin, or crop the back at the exact box. Picture: shots/r5-fliplayer-slack.png.
