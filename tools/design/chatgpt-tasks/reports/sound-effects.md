# CC0 / public-domain sound candidates for FreeMotion

Research date: 1 October 2026. Research only; no sound files were downloaded or added to the repository.

## What exists today

The Audio tab in [`js/addmenu.js`](../../js/addmenu.js) offers Import audio, Sound effects, Record voice, and imported audio entries. The built-in effects in `js/sfx.js` are generated in the browser rather than sampled files. Their current groups are **Movement, Impact, Build, Interface, Texture, and Nature**. The source comments explain this choice as a way to avoid uncertain licenses and bundle weight. REQUESTS.md already covers the sound-effects library and expansion, and separately requests cartoon sounds, beeps, transitions/glitches, ambience, foley, and other SFX ideas. This report is a sourced-candidate inventory for that existing work, not a new implementation request.

## Reading this list

Each source link goes to the individual Freesound sound page or the originating Kenney pack page; for pack entries the exact asset filename is given. The linked [CC0 1.0 legal text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) permits commercial use without attribution. Freesound pages below display the CC0 badge and file metadata. Kenney's official pack pages declare CC0; those pages do not publish per-file duration metadata, so I measured the listed OGG files from temporary copies outside the repo. Short subjective descriptions paraphrase the source description or filename/category and should be confirmed by audition before selecting a shipped set.

The recommendations are candidate sources, not a claim that every long ambience file should be bundled whole. No sound files were added to or downloaded into the repo.

## Excluded during screening

- I left out items whose page says CC BY or whose license/underlying sample was uncertain. Examples from the searches included `Click` by complex_waveform (CC BY), `Glitchy Static Noise` by PatrickLieberkind (CC BY), and `slide_whistle.wav` by plingativator (CC BY).
- I also left out `Noisy Whooshes` by qubodup from the main list: although its page shows CC0/public domain, the author specifically recommends linking the source page to avoid misunderstandings, so it does not fit this no-attribution shortlist cleanly.
- `Scary_Bell / Chime` by DRFX showed a CC0 badge but also text asking for a share of revenue above a threshold. That conflict makes its terms unclear, so it is excluded.
- Freesound metadata reflects each uploader's license selection; before bundling, audition the clip and keep the source/license snapshot with the selected asset. This is especially worthwhile for files with a long embedded sequence or references to source recordings.
