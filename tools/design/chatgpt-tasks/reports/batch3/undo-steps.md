## History model

`FM.history.commit()` compares the current scene snapshot with the current stack entry and returns without adding an entry when they are identical; otherwise it pushes one snapshot, drops the redo tail, and autosaves ([js/history.js:275-310](../../js/history.js#L275)). The implementation explicitly batches asynchronous multi-layer actions with a mute depth ([js/history.js:17-20](../../js/history.js#L17), [js/history.js:253-270](../../js/history.js#L253)). Undo and redo flush pending text/zoom edits before restoring one snapshot ([js/history.js:325-326](../../js/history.js#L325)).

## Mismatches found

No remaining action in this source read was confirmed to create several undo entries for one user action or to omit a required step. The multi-selection split and duplicate paths explicitly batch their internal commits; unchanged trim/drag/keyframe gestures avoid adding a no-op step. This is a **source-read conclusion only**, not a claim that every desktop and phone interaction was executed. Full interaction testing is **UNVERIFIED**.

The bounded history can eventually evict older actions: it retains at most 120 snapshots and trims further above 48,000,000 serialized characters while keeping at least eight ([js/history.js:298-302](../../js/history.js#L298)). This is a deliberate history limit, not an action-count mismatch; after enough edits, the oldest operation is no longer undoable.
