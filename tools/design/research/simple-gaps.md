# R5: what a CapCut beginner looks for first that Simple mode does not have yet

Looked at: Simple mode on `980-p22-r3` (a51b5e1d, v17.24), walked at 380 px in my container (the four tutorials of T9), plus the code. Line numbers marked *tip* are on that branch; unmarked ones are `origin/main` 2e3fd7a9. **What CapCut does** comes from web search snippets (not the app, which I cannot run here) and my own memory of it; the sources are listed at the bottom and are SEO-grade for the most part, so treat the CapCut column as "what the guides teach", not a measured product audit. **Full has it?** and **Simple now** are Read or Walked, and said so.

## What Simple has today (Walked / Read)
Clip row with the **+**; bottom row **Clips, Text, Sound (music, sound effects, record voice), Overlay**; the selected item's tray (**Length, Move earlier/later, Lift off, Duplicate, Crop, More, Delete**); scissors; undo and redo; export (simple-tools.js:186-191 *tip*; walked). **"More" opens the Full inspector's categories: Colouring, Outline & Shadows, Mixing, Position / Scale, Speed, Volume, Customise Shape, Presets, Effects** (walked, `more-panel-in-simple.jpg`), so several gaps below are "expose what is one tap deeper", not "build". The file header says **Phase 3 adds Captions, Look for all, Effects and Ask** to the bottom row (simple-tools.js:7-8 *tip*), so gaps 1 and 3 are already planned.

## The ten, in the order a beginner reaches for them
| # | Gap | How CapCut does it | Full already has it? | Simple now | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Auto captions** | Text, then Auto captions: speech becomes subtitles, editable, styled | yes: Detect speech (captions-vad.js, captions.js; app.js `detectSpeech` path; tutorial 12) | a Captions unit already has a tray (simple-tools.js:224 *tip*) but nothing creates one; **planned for Phase 3** (simple-tools.js:7) | M: a button that runs Full's detector, plus 2 to 3 caption looks |
| 2 | **Transitions between clips** | tap the small square between two clips, pick one | effects such as Dissolve (compositor.js:685) and per-layer Fade in / Fade out (inspector.js:5648-5649 *tip*); Simple's engine already models a crossfade seam (`kind: 'blend'`, spine-edit.js:487-488, :537-539 *tip*) | it can keep, shorten or close an existing one but **has no command that makes one** (the Simple edit commands are del, trim, split, closeSeam, duplicate, move, lift, intoRow, stay, z, endWithVideo, addText, length; Read of spine-edit.js:916-1441 *tip*) | M: one "Fade between" choice on the seam chip; the seam code is there |
| 3 | **A filter for the whole video, "apply to all"** | Filters tab, pick a look, a strength slider, Apply to all | yes: Presets, Colouring and Effects per layer, and an **Adjustment** layer that grades everything below it (addmenu 'Adjustment'; compositor.js:18087 `walk` over adjustment layers) | per clip only, through More; **"Look for all" planned for Phase 3** (simple-tools.js:7) | S to M: an adjustment layer behind a "Look for all" button, with a strength slider |
| 4 | **Speed, then speed curves** | Speed, Normal or Curve; presets (montage, hero time, flash in/out) | yes: Speed and keyframed speed ramps (inspector.js:5931-5959 *tip*) | basic Speed is **in More** (walked) but not in the tray; no ramp presets | S for a Speed button in the tray; M for 3 ramp presets |
| 5 | **Text looks and animations** | text templates, styles, in/out animations | yes: styles and animations (tutorial 11; text animation list inspector.js:5738 *tip*, "Fade in, Fade up, Typewriter, Pop...") | Text opens the editor (walked); style and animation only through More | S to M: a row of 6 looks and a few animations in the Text flow |
| 6 | **Fit, fill or blurred background when a clip's shape does not match** | Canvas / Background: blur or colour behind the video | yes: Backfill effect (compositor.js:1393) and the canvas settings (reachable from the cog in Simple, walked) | Backfill only through More, Effects | S: one "Fill the frame" choice that adds Backfill |
| 7 | **Music: fade in and out** | tap the music, Fade, drag | per-layer Fade in / Fade out exist (inspector.js:5648-5649 *tip*); **I did not check that they act on a music layer** | not in the tray (More, then Volume exists) | S if the fade applies to sound; M if it does not |
| 8 | **A music library** | Sounds tab with free tracks | FreeMotion has a sound-effects pack (Simple's "Sound effects" opens it) but **no music library** (Read: Add menu Audio is "from your files") | music only from the phone's files | L: content, licensing and a browser; not a code problem |
| 9 | **Stickers and emoji** | Stickers tab, tap to place | yes: Elements browser (addmenu.js:238 'Elements'; Home's Elements tab) | **Overlay only picks a video or photo from the phone** (simple-tools.js:189 *tip*: `pick('video/*,image/*')`), so no built-in stickers, shapes or arrows | M: an Overlay menu with "From your files" and "Stickers" using Full's browser |
| 10 | **Start from a template** | Templates tab: pick one, drop in your clips | yes: Home Templates tab (index.html:724) and the Add menu's Template | not reachable from inside Simple, and a template is built in Full's layers | M to L: depends on whether a template arrives as a clip row (Guess: most would not) |

Also asked for by beginners and **left out on purpose** of the ten: **background removal / AI cutout** (FreeMotion has Chroma Key, compositor.js:85, not an AI cutout: large and model-bound), **text to speech** (the browser speaks aloud but cannot be recorded into an export: not a small job), **stabilise**, **keyframes** (CapCut teaches them after the basics; Simple's job is to not need them), **reverse and freeze frame** (reverse is in Full, inspector.js:6056-6063 *tip*; I found no freeze-frame feature; small value for a first video).

## What I would do first (my opinion)
1. **Captions and Look for all**: already planned, and they are the two most "wow in one tap" features for a first video. Do them as planned.
2. **Speed in the tray, Fill the frame, text looks**: three small buttons that expose what More already holds; they make Simple feel complete for little code.
3. **Transitions**: the biggest real hole with the least visible workaround, and the engine half exists.
4. Stickers and music fades next; the music library and templates are content problems and can wait.

## Limits
CapCut's feature set and menu names change often and I read guides, not the app. I did not count which features beginners use most; none of the guides I found gave a ranking. Every "Full has it" was read as present in code; I did not test that each one works inside a Simple project.
