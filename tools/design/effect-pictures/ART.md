# FreeMotion effect tiles: art direction

## 1. What reads as AI or childish now

Generated work clusters at the statistical middle. Named tells: neon on dark, radial halos behind the subject, every area filled, smooth golden light, over-symmetry, identical weight across a grid, flat vector mixed with fake 3D. The current set has all of them.

- **One recipe, repeated.** Centred prop, dark vignette, soft halo: Vignette sunflower, Colour Cycle butterfly, Shake clock, Pulse heart, Orbit planet, Music Visualiser speaker.
- **Mascots and toys.** Panda, frog, cat, koi, robot, donut, lollipop, pinwheel, beach ball, cartoon TV, treasure map.
- **Synthwave default.** Purple-to-orange sky, sun, silhouettes: Wipe, Chroma Key, VHS Tape, Pixel Sort, Directional Blur, Dispersion.
- **Sticker gloss.** Thick outlines, gold frames, sparkles: Stroke Colour, Number Roll, Word Highlight, Pulse, Swing.
- **Neon tubes.** Live Wire, Shockwave, Scramble Text, Flicker, Starfield.
- **Illustrated, not performed.** Blinds paints a pretty beach through slats; Duotone draws a skater instead of mapping a photograph to two colours.
- **No rest.** Nothing is cropped or empty; every tile has equal weight.

## 2. FreeMotion's identity, in rules

**World.** An editor's bench, from take 13: sheets of a comp, mattes, playheads, rulers, timecode, bezier handles, waveforms.

**Grounds.** Bone `#e7e2d5`, Ink `#16130f`, Coal `#0c0c0d`; UI navy `#161c28`, hairline `#2b3547`, text `#eef2f8`, dim `#8b96a8`. Cream-by-default is itself a listed tell: Bone on at most a third of tiles.

**Accents, one per tile.** Vermilion `#e4471d`, keyframe yellow `#ffce4a`, teal `#29d9bb`. Teal on dark is close to Alight Motion and the "cyan on dark" tell: controls only, never glow or fill.

**Labels.** Courier Prime bold caps on a flat tab, as in take 13: `CLIP 01`, `MATTE`. Fonts: `"Courier Prime", monospace` or 800 system sans.

**Light.** One off-centre point. No halos.

**Motion.** Physical and decisive: sheets part, settle with damped overshoot, a matte slams flat. Long holds, hard cuts. No sine-wave floating.

**Truth (his "can it actually look like that?").**
- Source art can be anything; the change must be real: real maths on real pixels, at a normal setting, never the maximum.
- Furniture (tabs, handles, scopes, film rebate) sits outside the picture or is unmistakably UI. `PLAY` burned into a VHS image claims the effect prints it.
- Loop: source, effect, hold, back.

**Photographs.** 11 of 18 are golden-hour, the next sameness: one per section at most. Favour huracan, mclaren, revuelto, tesla, towers, bush, cat. They are 320 px: never crop tighter than half-frame. Plates and badges must not read unless the tile is about them.

**Size.** The menu thumb is 62 px tall in a 4-across grid. Judge at 80 px.

## 3. Ten directions

Fixed across all: square picture, one material, at most three colours plus any photograph. Everything else varies, the way Penguin Great Ideas made each cover its own inside a two-colour system.

**A. Graded Still** (colourist's before/after). Palette: the photograph's only. Texture: none added. Type: none, or one tab. Moves: one parameter travels, by Bone hairline split or stepped A/B; hold 40% of the loop. Suits: grades, blurs.

**B. Comp Sheets** (take 13). Bone, Ink, Coal, one accent. Flat sheets, 1-unit edge, a Courier tab each. Moves: sheets part in depth, overshoot, matte drops. Suits: mattes, wipes, flips.

**C. Contact Sheet** (darkroom proof). Coal, paper `#d9d4c6`, chinagraph red `#d8321f`. Film rebate, sprockets, silver grain, Courier frame numbers `12A`. Moves: steps frame to frame at 12 fps; a wobbling red circle marks the pick, an X strikes a reject. Suits: time, sequences, side-by-side proofs.

**D. Two-Ink Print** (Riso, Blue Note). Bone plus exactly two inks: vermilion and Ink, or `#1f7f70` and `#f6c544`. Overprint with `multiply`, plates 0.7 units out of register, speckle. One big undisturbed colour area; asymmetric crop; heavy sans caps. Moves: one plate slides against the other, on twos. Suits: dots, patterns, two-colour maps.

**E. Type Poster** (International Style). One field: Bone on Ink, or Ink on vermilion. No image. Heavy sans fills 70% and runs off an edge, flush left. Moves: only the letters perform; hard cuts. Suits: text, timing.

**F. Viewer Overlay** (the app's own gizmos). Navy, 0.5-unit `#8b96a8` hairlines, teal square anchors, yellow keyframe diamond, around a photograph. Every line measures or controls something; decorative grids are a listed tell. Courier values: `R 34`, `72°`. Moves: a handle drives the picture, eased, then holds. Suits: warps, optics, 3D, tracked regions.

**G. Cut Paper** (Saul Bass). Three flat take-13 colours. Scissor-straight edges, slightly untrue angles. No gradient, shading or jagged "torn" fringe (another listed tell). One adult form: arm, eye, arrow, clapper, reel. Moves: on threes, small rotation change between poses. Suits: movement.

**H. Broadcast Bench** (master control). True black, 75% colour bars, `#eef2f8`. Real instruments: waveform, vectorscope, safe-title marks, lower third. Courier timecode `00:04:11:03`. Moves: ticks at 25 fps, no easing, sudden drop-outs. Suits: video damage, keys, captions.

**I. Chronophotograph** (Marey, Muybridge). Black and silver only. Ruled backdrop with real scale; exposures accumulate on one plate. Moves: strobed, one exposure per beat, hold, then end on the single layer moving live, so nobody reads ghosts as output. Suits: shakes, orbits.

**J. Pen Plotter** (1960s computer art). Bone with ink and one red pen, or Coal with white pen. Constant 0.5-unit line, no fill or glow, a small ink dot where strokes start. `FIG. 3`. Moves: draws at pen speed, pen-up jumps visible, holds finished. Suits: edges, generators.

## 5. Anti-AI checklist

1. Swap the prop: could this be another tile? Redo.
2. Mascot, toy, smiling object? Cut.
3. Halo, gradient sky, gradient text, sparkle? Remove unless the effect makes it.
4. Dead-centre and symmetrical? Crop off an edge, unless the effect needs a centre.
5. A fifth of the tile quiet?
6. Can you say why each colour is there?
7. One material only.
8. Every line and number true?
9. Beside the app's real render, at a normal setting: same result?
10. Furniture clearly furniture?
11. A hold, a hit, a stepped beat?
12. Reads at 80 px? Differs from both neighbours?
