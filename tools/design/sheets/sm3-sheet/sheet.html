8 · Just look

Look: filters and Adjust for one clip

Look in a clip’s tools opens its own panel: under the tools on a phone, above them in the left band on a PC.

  • Filters: None first, then your favourites, your saved filters and the library. Strength sits under them once a filter is on. In the picture: {{look_picked}}.
  • Adjust: Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Exposure, Warmth and Vignette, then Reset. Each runs from −100 to +100 (Vignette 0 to 100). Brightness +100 is half as bright again, Saturation +100 is double, Exposure +100 is two stops brighter.
  • Warmth is the word used for colour temperature.
  • What it says as you go: “Noir on Clip 2”, “Strength 40%”, “Brightness +50”, “Adjust is back to zero”.
  • Polaroid, Kodachrome and Technicolor now show in Simple too (Full already shows them). Noted for the list before publishing; nothing to decide now.

9 · Just look

Captions, Effects and the bottom row

  • The bottom row is now six: Clips, Text, Captions, Sound, Overlay and Effects. About {{row380_w}} px each on a phone and {{row1280_w}} px on a PC; {{row_cut}}.
  • Captions opens Type captions, Find speech and Style. Find speech finds when people talk, and you type the words. With nothing selected it listens to every clip.
  • Edit lines lists every line with its time. Tap a line to type into it, ✕ removes it, and an empty line reads “{{cap_empty}}”.
  • The words say captions, never cues. Find speech answers with a line you can read, like “2 captions from Clip 2 · type their words”.
  • An effect over a clip has Change effect, Strength, Stay put, More and the bin. Strength fades the whole effect, 0 to 100%. A new one lasts 3 s, or to the end of its clip.
  • The picker is Simple’s own. Effects that need a clip under them are left out of it for now.
  • What it says: “Invert over Clip 2”, “Changed to Grayscale”, “Deleted the effect”, “Add a clip first, then an effect over it”.
