3 · Phone and PC

A saved element of one picture and a sound: should they be tied together?

The plan said such an element makes no group. Then nothing remembers which sound came with which picture: a whoosh just past a cut rode the next clip, and moving either clip pulled them apart. As built they are tied, the way a lower third’s pieces are, and the sound’s tools say so:

A

Tied in a group, the picture leads{{rec:3A}}

As built. The same rule a lower third already uses. In Full it is an ordinary closed group named after the element, and Ungroup there unties it.

B

No group: each sound linked to the picture as its parent{{rec:3B}}

Full would show the sound with a Parent set: a stored link the plan argues against.

C

The group, but whichever starts first leads{{rec:3C}}

A whoosh that starts before the picture would lead, and a sound that runs on more than a second past its clip would then make the whole element stay put.

Why the recommended one: One rule for every element, nothing new in Full, and Ungroup undoes it.

5 · Just look

Extras: shapes, your saved elements and templates in Simple

Clips is now a short list: Add clips (what Clips did before) and Extras. The Clips button’s full name, for a screen reader and the hover on a PC, is “{{clips_title}}”.

On a phone Extras is the Add sheet you know, with only three tabs ({{x_tabs}}). Its title still reads Add: it is Full’s own sheet, and changing that word would change Full.

A saved lower third goes in as one piece that moves together, and each part keeps its own tools. Its words say what they move with:

  • Stay put on the words is named “{{x_with_stay}}” for a screen reader.
  • With nothing saved, Elements says “{{x_empty_title}}”: {{x_empty_lines}}
  • A shape, an element or a template is one tap and one Undo step. What it says: “Shape added”, “‘Lower third’ added”, “‘Promo’ added · 2 clips in the row”.
  • A template’s clips go into the clip row at the cut nearest the line; an element never does. An element’s camera move is left out in Simple, and the line says so.

6 · Just look

Insert your Media over Simple: a shorter clip closes up

When a template’s project is in Simple (question 2), Replace Media on Insert your Media is Simple’s own Replace: a shorter clip keeps its place, the clips after it move up with no gap, and a title on it stays on it. In Full it is today’s Replace, which leaves a gap.

Insert your Media covers the whole screen, so when Simple has something to say it says it on the sheet, between the slots and Replace Media:

The whole PC screen
  • Do it anyway swaps the clip in one step: the locked clip moves up and stays locked.
  • Open in Full puts Full under the sheet; you press Replace Media again there and pick the file again.
  • A clip inside a group (a photo in a masking frame) only takes a clip at least as long as its place in the group.
