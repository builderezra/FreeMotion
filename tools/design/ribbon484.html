Silk Ribbon — three shapes

Your #484 clause 2's last item. You asked whether a flowing ribbon should be a shape (A), an effect (B) or skipped (C). Under rule 16 it is built as a shape beside Banner and Flag — these are the three outlines, drawn by the app's own curve walker, large and at the 24px the Add menu shows.

A — Streamer (recommended, shipped)
A wave with swallowtail ends. Reads as a ribbon at 24px because the notched ends are the one thing a banner does not have.
B — Sash
One arc, straight-cut ends. Calmer, but at 24px it reads as a smile or a bridge rather than a ribbon.
C — Twist
Pinched in the middle to suggest a twist. Works large; at 24px the pinch closes up and it reads as a bow tie.
Shipped as A under Add → Shape → Silk ribbon, beside Banner and Flag, in v15.73. To change it, say ribbon B or ribbon C; to have it as an effect instead, say ribbon effect; to drop it, ribbon skip. Points can be edited like any shape.
