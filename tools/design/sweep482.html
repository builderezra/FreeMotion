Ten categories swept by number: 155 effects, one fix

After Colouring and Warping by eye, the rest of the catalogue went through the same sheet tool by number only: every effect rendered by the app on its section's photograph at your phone's frame size, at its defaults, at a quarter of its main slider and at the top, and each tile scored by how far it moved the picture. Rows that scored near zero were then looked at one by one. Blur 19 · Generative 19 · Stylize 20 · 3D 18 · Drawing 16 · Keying 11 · Opacity 7 · Repetition 6 · Movement 7. Text was skipped: its effects only act on text layers.

The fix

Electric Edges fixed in v15.84

Cyan lightning along the edges of the picture. On a photograph it drew nothing you could see at any setting: its lines key on how hard an edge is, a photograph's edges are soft, and the maths was linear, so soft edges scored a few percent. It had been made visible once before, on a test picture with hard edges, which is exactly the trap: a test picture can pass an effect that real footage cannot see.

Now a Soft edges control, on by default, bends the response so a soft edge registers while a hard edge stays exactly as strong as before. Projects saved before this keep their old look. Left three: whole photograph. Right three: a 1:1 crop of the same three renders.

Swipe sideways for the crops. The numbers are how far each render moved the picture, on a 0 to 255 scale, at 1080 px.

Zeros that are not faults

On a still photograph that fills the frame, a whole family of effects has nothing to act on. Each was checked against its own description.

No edge to work on

Inner Glow, Roughen Edges, Smooth Bevel, Smooth Edges, Stroke Colour, Matte Choker, Edge Halo, Depth Push, Drop Shadow. They act on the layer's outline, and a full-frame photograph has none inside the frame. On text and shapes they work as before.

No motion, no time

Motion Blur (Footage), Motion Blur (Object), Temporal Denoise, Frame Stutter, Trail, and Blink at its "on" moment. One still frame gives them nothing to compare or hold.

Needs a second layer

Compound Blur, Backfill, Light Wrap, Luma Matte, and Chroma Key on a photograph with no green in it.

By construction at the top of the slider

Tiles' copies land outside a full frame (the rule from #582), a Wipe or Radial Wipe at progress 1 is the whole picture, Mirror's seam at 100% is the edge, Shockwave's ring has left the frame.

Looked at and left

Zoom Streaks

Reads 2.8 at its maximum on a daytime photograph. It is a light streak: it weights what it streaks by brightness squared, so lights against dark streak hard and a blown daytime sky has nothing to streak. That is its design, and Spin Streaks (a plain smear) is the one to reach for on daytime footage.

Squircle Corners

Its Radius slider reads constant because the "Continuous curve" toggle is on by default and its note says radius does nothing while it is on.

VHS Tape

Reads 5.4 at most, and at 1:1 that is real tape wobble tearing the building edges. Subtle by nature.

What to reply

Nothing, if this is fine. The Electric Edges fix is shipped.

The by-eye pass (a picture per effect, judged for look, not just for life) has covered Colouring and Warping. Name the next category to do that way, or "no" to stop; the two Colouring taste calls (Gradient Overlay, Palette Map) still wait on their own page.
