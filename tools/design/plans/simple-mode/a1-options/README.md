# A1 as pictures: a video clip's tray on a PC

A video clip's tray has 13 tools (14 with Close gap). The PC band is 306 px wide, so his pick B (two rows, every tool on show) cannot hold it: it went back to one scrolling row. Open `sheet.jpg` on the phone; the full screenshots are beside it. Rendered in the real app (release 2.3 on `hunt/simple-2.3`, a scratch switch `FM._a1` per option, Chromium 1194), a real video with sound, Simple, the clip selected.

| | option | tools | rows | what the pictures show |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | as built | 13 | one, scrolling | Move later, Lift off, Replace, Reverse past the edge |
| B | a third row | 13 | three | band 52 px taller; Move later and Reverse still cut off; at 1024x600 the words above squeeze |
| C | "Sound & speed" as S1 wrote it | **11** | one, scrolling | **the S1 doc said ten; it is eleven**, so it does not get two rows |
| D | Reverse and Replace behind More | 11 | one, scrolling | and More has no Replace, so it would vanish from Simple |
| **E** | **one "Audio" tool (Speed, Volume, Reverse, Take sound out) (recommended)** | **10** | **two** | every tool on show at both sizes; Replace kept |

**Recommended: E.** It is the only one that keeps his pick B for the commonest clip and loses nothing. The cost: Speed and Volume are one tap further (Audio, then the tool). I also tried E with the label "Sound & speed": 338 px of buttons in a 306 px band, so Lift off and Delete were cut; the short word "Audio" fits exactly (306 of 306) at 1280 and 299 of 299 at 1024. If he dislikes "Audio", any label up to about 50 px works.
Not built beyond a scratch switch: this is for his answer first. The switch is on branch `s5-scratch` in my container only; the numbers are in the pictures.
