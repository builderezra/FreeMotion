# Simple mode, in plain words

Planning only. Revised 1 Oct to your three rules, then again to your answers of the same day. You said **build it** (D15 A),
so Phase 1 can start; the parts of it you can see wait for the six questions at the bottom.

**Your original editor stays exactly as it is.** Full keeps every button, word, menu, key and behaviour it has today: its
play bar, its ⋯ strip, Home, New project, Settings, how its edits and undo work, what it exports, and how it behaves with a
friend in. Every part of the plan that changed Full has been taken out, or kept inside Simple only. Before any Simple update
ships, a check compares Full against the version before it, screen by screen and edit by edit, and the update is refused if
anything in Full is different. Where one of your answers would have changed Full, it is not used and the question is asked
again (D3, D14b below).

**The honest costs** (measured on the real app, 1 Oct). The cog's new section takes room: on your phone held upright nothing
moves; on shorter phones Canvas settings sits lower and scrolls a bit sooner (Apply always stays); on a computer the Canvas ⇄
Friends swap shows the new tile moving too. Turned sideways, the new section lands off the screen, which is not solved yet
(D24). Two more, only with a friend in: both devices need the same FreeMotion version to join (as today after any update), and
once Simple can edit, ⌘Z on a step made in Simple can be refused in Full while a friend can edit.

**What it is.** A second editor in FreeMotion, called **Simple**. Today's editor is **Full**. Those are the names on screen
everywhere (your D1). Like CapCut: one row of clips end to end, with text, captions, overlays and sound in their own rows.
Delete, trim or move a clip and the rest close up. Titles stick to the clip they start on; music and voice-overs stay put.

**Switching is in the ⚙ cog.** The cog gets a third section beside Canvas settings and Friends. It stays small: a
**Simple ⇄ Full** switch and a **What should you use?** button. Tap the switch and you're in the other editor. Tap **What
should you use?** and the section opens big, the same way Canvas settings and Friends do, with a short explanation of each
editor. Nothing else switches: no new button on either play bar, no E key. One project, two views; nothing is converted, and
switching never changes your project. When you switch, the clips **morph** into place as the cog closes: the one animation
you picked (D11). Fold and Slide are dropped.

**A warning first if a switch would lose anything.** Switching never writes to your project. The only things it could lose are
unfinished work in an open tool: a crop or touch-up you haven't applied, a drawing you haven't finished, or redo steps. (With
the voice recorder open, it waits until you close it.) In those cases it asks first (*"Your crop isn't applied yet… Apply crop and switch / Stay"*);
Stay leaves everything exactly as it was.

**Music longer than your clips** (your D17 B): the video runs on in black, as Full does today. Simple never cuts or fades
your music to fit, and there is no "Ends with the video" switch. To end a song with the video, trim its end.

**On a computer** (your D20 A): a tool's panel (Look, Speed, Captions) opens inside the left band, under the picture. The
trade-off: at 1280×800 it has about 100 px before it scrolls, about half the room it would have had over the timeline, so
long panels scroll sooner. In return the picture and the timeline are never covered. A phone held sideways has no room in the
band, so there the panel opens over the timeline.

**Old projects.** Open in Simple straight away. Simple works out the clip row and writes nothing until you first move clips.

**Working together.** You in Full, a friend in Simple, same project, same time. Looks, text, captions and sound work live
(your D14 A). Moving clips while a friend who can edit is connected is **on hold**: the version we designed would change how
Full behaves with a friend in, so it waits for your answer (D14b).

**What gets built now.** First a lock that refuses any Simple update that changes Full. Then the engine (nothing on screen).
Then Phase 1 itself: any project shown as clips, read-only, with the switch in the cog. That last part ships once D18, D22,
D23 and D24 are answered. Then editing, then looks and captions, then transitions.
