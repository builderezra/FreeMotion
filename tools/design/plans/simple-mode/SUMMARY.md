# Simple mode, in plain words

Planning only. Nothing is built until you say so.

**What it is.** A second editor in FreeMotion, called **Simple** for now (Quick is an option in D1). Like CapCut: one row
of clips end to end, with text, captions, overlays and sound in their own rows. Delete, trim or move a clip and the rest
close up. Titles stick to the clip they start on; music and voice-overs stay put. Today's editor stays as it is, called
**Full**.

**Switching.** One project, two views. Nothing is converted. One button on the play bar (E on a keyboard) flips between
them, keeping your place, selection and undo, and writing nothing. What Simple can't edit still plays exactly as made, with
"Open in Full" beside it.

**Old projects.** Open in Simple straight away. Simple works out the clip row and writes nothing until you first move
clips. A wrong guess is one tap to fix. Gaps from Full show as a block you tap to close; Simple never tidies by itself.

**Working together.** You in Full, a friend in Simple, same project, same time. Looks, text, captions and sound work live
from the first editing release. Moving clips while a friend can edit comes in the "together" release, after your first
real Mac and iPhone test. A friend's things are never deleted behind their back.

**Built first.** Phase 1: any project shown as clips, plus the switch, behind a Settings switch (off until you turn it
on). Then editing, then looks and captions, then working together, then transitions.
