# More choice: what's coming when the list runs dry

You said this is the complex version and more choice is always better (#966). So I went through every effect, filter and sound in FreeMotion and compared them with CapCut, Alight Motion, After Effects and Premiere. This is what came out.

## The next three updates (nothing to answer, these just get built)

**1 · Looks you already use get more say**
- **Film Grain** can run fast, slow or frozen, go softer, and switch between 8mm, 16mm, 35mm and 65mm film. It'll also look the same on your phone as in the export (right now it's chunkier on the phone).
- **Light Leak** can hold still or drift faster. It gets a second colour and a flicker.
- **Glow** gets a Strength slider.
- **Letterbox**: pick the bars by shape (cinema 2.39:1, 4:5, 1:1…), and fade or slide them in.
- **Warmth** that keeps blacks black, **Colour Balance** that doesn't brighten the picture, and a colour for **Faded Film**'s fade.

**2 · Shake, wiggle and loops**
- **Shake and Wiggle** get a Pattern so two layers stop shaking in sync, plus rotation and zoom wobble. "Hide edges" means a shaking clip never shows black corners.
- A **heartbeat** pulse, a **swing** that settles, **orbits** on an oval, and a **ticker** that wraps round the screen.
- **Flash (darken)** can hit on a steady beat. **Speed lines** flicker like anime. **Glitch** gets chunky block damage.

**3 · Sounds**
- The ▶ on sound effects plays exactly what gets added, and turns into ■ so you can stop it.
- Searching audio effects finds "karaoke", "robot", "bass boost" and "muffled".
- **Echo** gets darker with each repeat, like tape.
- **Reverb** gets a gap before the room kicks in, and a brighter/darker tone.
- **Pitch** goes up or down two octaves, with fine-tune.
- **Bass & Treble** you can aim, a **Compressor** with its own volume and blend, and a **Limiter** that really stops sound going over the top.
