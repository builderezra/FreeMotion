## #1080 A song or file is added AT THE PLAYHEAD (LOW)
- **Claim: Verified (Measured).** `app.js:3055` `start = Math.min(FM.time, P.duration)`: with the playhead at the end of a 10 s project a song starts at 10 s, after the video, and lengthens the project by the song. For footage this is intended (the comment above it: clips butt onto the end instead of leaving a gap); for music it is the wrong end.
- **Callers traced (Read).** One function, `FM.addMediaLayer`, serves the picker, drops and the Add menu. A song is a "video" record with no picture (`width` and `height` 0), the rule the inspector already uses.
- **Patch (the report's first option).** A picture-less file added with the playhead at or past the end of a non-empty project starts at 0. Mid-video it still starts at the playhead; footage still butts onto the end. The second option in the report ("show where it landed") is a toast and could sit beside this.
- **Test.** `P21 #1080`: a song at 4 s starts at 4 (CONTROL), footage at the end starts at 10 (CONTROL), a song at the end starts at 0. **Mutations:** footage also at 0 (A1), a song always at 0 (A2), the old line (A3): all CAUGHT.
- **Open choice for him (ask):** whether the song at the end should start at 0 (recommended: it is music for the video) or stay put with a toast. Also **Guess:** a song added at the end of an EMPTY-tailed project with `duration` 0 is unchanged (the patch needs a non-empty project).

## #1081 PC export: Save may open the OS Share window with no way to save the MP4 on Windows (MEDIUM)
- **Claim: Verified in code, Windows behaviour not run.** `exporter.js:66` `deliver()` shares whenever `navigator.canShare({files})` is true, with no device check. **Measured here:** with `canShare` true and a mouse pointer, Save went to `navigator.share` and made no download (test red). That desktop Chrome / Edge on Windows answer `canShare` true for files, and that the Windows share window has no "save to disk", is the report's claim and I have no Windows machine here: **Guess**, but cheap to check on his laptop (export, Save).
- **Callers traced (Read).** `deliver` is called only from the export result's `save` (`exporter.js:1623`, `1626`), which the ready card's Save button calls (`app.js:6305`). Nothing else decides share vs download.
- **Patch.** Share only when the primary pointer is coarse (`matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')`): phones and iPads keep the sheet, mouse devices download. Plus the seam `FM._deliver` for the test.
- **Options (ask):** (1) as patched, a PC downloads (recommended: deterministic, nothing to explain); (2) a PC downloads AND the ready card gets a "Share…" button for macOS Safari users who like AirDrop. (2) is a UI change.
- **Test.** `P21 #1081`: coarse pointer shares once and does not download (CONTROL); fine pointer downloads and does not share. **Mutations:** PC shares (B1), phone downloads (B2): both CAUGHT.
- **Risk.** A touch laptop whose primary pointer is the trackpad downloads, which is what we want; an iPad with a keyboard still reports coarse and shares.
