**Three rules:**
1. **One level on screen.** `#sm-bar[data-mode]` = `project` | `item` | `sub` | `panel`.
2. **Row or panel.** It fits one 56 px row (a few choices, or one slider) → **sub-row**. It needs a grid, tabs, a list or a
   keyboard → **panel**.
3. **One sheet line.** On a phone every panel (Full's, Simple's, AI) runs from `FM.mobile.sheetLine()` (#stage's bottom,
   F3/F10's line) to the bottom, and Simple's stage never shrinks. On a PC Simple's panels open in the left band (D20 A).

## (b) Conflicts

**1. Look lives on the project row only.**
- **Decision:**
  - Look is project-wide (S12) and keeps the tabs Filters · Adjust · Quality.
  - Quality keeps only whole-frame tools: Crisper, Less grain and Even light.
  - S20's other tiles move: Auto colour duplicates S19's Auto, so it goes. Steady moves to the tray, and Smooth motion into
    Speed. Sharpen for 4K is an export option for S6, later.
  - The only per-clip colour tool is Match (S19).
  - S12's step-0 probe also renders `temporaldenoise` and `deflicker` on the look layer. Whichever fails becomes a per-clip
    tool.
  - S35 never keys Look, because the look layer is never selected.
- **Why:** "you shouldn't have the look menu on each layer… goes over your entire project immediately". His S18 (filters, then
  adjust, then video quality, saved together) also puts Quality in that panel.
- **Plan sections that change:**
  - A COMBINED §4 (the item list): replaced by (a).
  - A S5 step 4: the hint drops "or look".
  - B S28 step 1: no Look.
  - F S20 change 1 and tests: project-wide, always shown, three tiles.
  - A S35 change 2: "Look's six adjust sliders" removed.
  - C S18: `quality` holds those three tiles.

**2. One tray order, as in (a).**
- **Why:** S33 puts "your main section with… everything I've said so far" first, then groups with a line between. S28 puts
  Speed, Volume, Reverse and Take sound out in the main section, so they are not repeated later (Extract audio IS Take sound
  out). S32 wants Replace in the first four. S31 puts Delete last, scrolling.
- **Plan sections that change:**
  - A COMBINED, B S28 step 1 and B S32 step 1: replaced by (a).
  - F S33 change 1: five sections plus Delete. Flip and Turn fold into Transform.
  - C S30: the face is "Effects".
  - B S10 change 2: becomes S30's first cut.

**3. One sub-row mechanism: A S9's.**
- **Decision:**
  - `#sm-bar[data-mode]` with one `subFor = {key, id}` state and one `SUBS` table. `ROWS`/`rowFor` fold into it.
  - Sub-rows render in `#sm-tray` through `strip()`; `#sm-tools` shows only in `project` mode.
  - S8's 180 ms slide stays as the mode swap, with no slide under reduced motion.
  - `openMenu` is deleted once S5 and S6 remove its last callers.
  - The contents are in (a).
- **Why:** one state serves both project and tray sub-rows (Speed, Transform, Curve). S8's `#sm-tools[data-sub]` only covers
  the project row.
- **Plan sections that change:**

  - B S8's list: Templates leaves Clips (see 4). Shape stays in Extras (his D21 A), not Overlay. Edit lines stays on the
    caption track's tray.

**4. Templates get their own project-row tool** (S21 A), opening a panel. Extras loses its Template tab, and B S8's Clips row

  - Anchor, axis and tilt are **Fine-tune**, which waits for F6. Nothing is shared with Full's Move & Transform.

**10. One normaliser: `S.fitProjectLayers(R2)`, in `js/spine-edit.js` beside `fitTails`.**
- **Decision:**
  - It runs at the end of EVERY `S.edit` step, and is idempotent.
  - Cases, in order:
    1. `sm.bg` image at the bottom, spanning [first clip start, trackEnd];
    2. with `project.sm.bg.kind === 'blur'`, exactly one sm-1 `fillbehind` per main clip;
    3. whole-video segments marked **`sm.whole`** (cleared when either end is dragged), same span;
    4. `sm.look` layer, same span, directly above the top-most video or picture.
  - It never writes in Full. On entering Simple, one line covers all four: "N project layers end before your last clip · Fix".
  - `look`, `bg` and `whole` join `SM_FLAGS`, the sanitiser and the collab fixture in the release that first writes each.
- **Why:** three plans each kept a layer at the project's length. A flag is how two of them already worked, and S10's derived
  "[0, old end]" rule would need the old end inside the pass.
- **Plan sections that change:** B S10 change 1; C S12 change 4 (`fitLook` becomes case 4); C S22 change 1.
