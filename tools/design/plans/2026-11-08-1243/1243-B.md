### #1243 S1: the transition marker clips with the layer above

**His words:** S1 🐞✏️ "When you split a layer… it puts a little blue thing above it, but that blue thing clips with whatever is on the above layer, like if you have text… a pretty shitty spot to put your… transition thing."

**What's there now:**

- It sits in `#sm-trlane`, which is created before `#sm-sections` (`simple-timeline.js:75-76`). Its top is `mainEl.offsetTop - 36` (`:660`), which puts it inside the lowest 32 px section lane, exactly where a title, overlay or Behind item sits.
- The CSS deliberately paints it UNDER those items (`z-index: auto`, `styles.css:12529-12542`). So a title in that lane covers half of it.
- Cause, from reading: the position, nothing else.
- A tap selects the clip after the cut and opens its Transition row (`:680`).

**The change (A):**
1. Draw the marker in `#sm-main` (the clip row), centred on the cut both ways: `top = (MAIN_H - 24) / 2`.
   - Visual: a 24×24 rounded square with our own glyph (two half-tiles meeting at a slanted seam). Not CapCut's bow-tie and not anything from AM.
   - Hit area: 44×44 through padding.
   - z-index 4: above the clips, below the grips (z 6, `styles.css:12784`).
2. When the clip on either side of a cut is selected, hide that cut's marker. Its trim grips (13 px caps outside each edge, `drawGrips` `:531-552`) own both edges.
   - Give that clip's tray a **Transition** tool instead. It opens the row for the cut before the clip, or the cut after it for the first clip.
3. A cut that has a transition shows that type's small icon in the marker (from S2), so he can tell which cuts have one.
4. Remove `#sm-trlane` (`:75-76`, `styles.css:12536`). Keep the sections' pointer-events rule at `:12541-12542`; the sections still need it.

**Tests (380 and 1280):**
- Two joined clips, with a title in the lowest lane spanning the cut.
- Assert: the marker's rect is inside `#sm-main`'s rect, its rect intersects no `#sm-sections .sm-item`, and `elementFromPoint` at its centre is the marker.
- Select clip 2: no marker at its cuts; the tray has `[data-tool=transition]`, which opens `#sm-tray[data-row=transition]`.
- **Mutation:** put the marker back at `mainEl.offsetTop - 36` in the lane, and the intersection and elementFromPoint checks go red.

**Options:**
- **A (RECOMMENDED):** on the cut inside the clip row, hidden while a neighbour is selected, with Transition in that clip's tray.
- **B:** its own thin 16 px "joins" lane between the clip row and the sound row. It never overlaps anything, but costs height out of the phone's 170 px timeline.

### #1243 S7: BUG: the Clips/Text/Captions/Sound buttons pushed up after switching

**His words:** S7 🐞 "switching back and forth between simple and complex, and now… the clips text caption sound buttons are all… pushed up for some reason."

**What's there now (phone):**
- `#sm-timeline` is absolute, from 40 px down to the bottom of `#timeline-panel` (`styles.css:12461-12464`).
- `#sm-scroll` is a fixed 170 px (`:12564`), followed by `#sm-bar` (`#sm-say` 52 px plus `#sm-tools` 56 px plus the safe area, `:12629`).
- The stage height is Simple's `--stage-h`: `:12556` with a sheet up, `:12563` without.
- Anything that leaves the stage shorter than Simple's no-sheet value lifts the whole timeline panel and leaves empty space under the tools. That is "pushed up".

**Likely causes, from reading (not measured), most likely first:**
1. **The stage pin** (`js/timeline.js:479-494`). A touch on Full's `#timeline` writes `--stage-h` inline on `#app` at Full's height (40svh) and removes it on pointerup or pointercancel. An inline value on `#app` beats `body.ed-simple`'s.
   - If the release is ever missed (the app goes to the background mid-touch, or iOS steals the gesture), Full's height sticks in Simple until a reload.
2. **A Simple panel survives the switch.** `sync()` (`simple-tools.js:1260`) and `onChange` (`:136-137`) clear the effects picker, Extras and the hint when Simple is left, but not `panelFor` or `panelKind`.
   - Back in Simple with the same selection, More or Look rises again (`sheetHeld` is false, `mobile.js:60`), which sets `body.insp-open` and makes the stage take the 514 px clamp.
3. **`body.insp-open` left on** (`mobile.js:23-24`) by a refresh during the switch that opens Full's sheet.

**The change:**
1. **Probe first.** A tests/ probe at 380×844 through `_cdp.py`, using REAL touch (`Input.dispatchTouchEvent`), because synthetic pointer events can't show capture.
   - Steps: hold on Full's timeline, then switch by the cog and by Open in Full, three times each, with and without a selection, and with More open.
   - Dump after each switch: the inline styles on `#app` and `<html>`, the body classes, the `#stage` height, the rects of `#timeline-panel` and `#sm-tools`, and `panelFor`.
2. **Fix what was measured.**
   - Cause 1: `FM.editor.onChange` removes the pin, and the pin also lets go on `visibilitychange` and `pagehide`.
   - Cause 2: clear `panelFor` and `panelKind` when leaving Simple.
   - Cause 3: close Full's sheet when entering Simple.
3. **A structural guard.** In Simple on a phone, an inline `--stage-h` on `#app` with no finger down is removed and reported once.

**Tests:**
- **380, real touch:** after N switches, the tools' bottom equals `#sm-timeline`'s bottom minus the safe area (±1), and `#app.style.getPropertyValue('--stage-h')` is `''`.
- A separate test for cause 2: open More, go to Full and back, and the sheet is closed with `panelFor` null.
- **1280:** unchanged.
- **Mutation:** remove the measured fix (the unpin on switch, or the `panelFor` reset), and it goes red.

**No pick needed.** **Size:** M. **Related:** G4 ("the bottom row… goes up and glitches" on opening a project) uses the same stage-height path; run the same probe on it.

### #1243 S11: BUG: stuck on one frame after adding an effect

**His words:** S11 🐞 "my thing is completely glitched right now… stuck on one frame and… nothing is like really working cleanly… And I just added like an effect." (Reproduce: Simple, one project, add an effect from the Effects menu.)

The class is cleared in a `finally` (`:1430`), but only once `await applyPlan` settles. A step that never settles leaves the whole editor dead, which is exactly "nothing works".
2. **The picker's thumbnails.** Every tile gets a live canvas (`simple-tools.js:946-956`). The tiles are generated one per animation frame (`fx-thumbs.js:1711-1729`), and the animated ones are repainted by a shared interval (`:1671-1691`). On a phone that competes with the preview for every frame.
3. **The adjustment layer's per-frame cost** at full resolution on iPhone (`compositor.js:30822` `applyAdjustment`). A heavy grade can drop the preview to about 1 fps, which looks frozen. Same path as G6 (#202).
4. **A render-loop exception** from the new instance.

**The change:**
1. Reproduce his shape at 380 (one video, Simple, Effects → each of the first 10 tiles) with `perf-probe.js`. For 5 s after the tap, playing and paused, record: `S.running`, `sm-running`, the queue length, the frame times, the fx-thumbs pending count and any console errors.
2. Fix the measured cause, plus two guards whatever the cause:
   - **A runner watchdog.** `applyPlan` races an 8 s timeout. On timeout: restore the document as it was before the edit (`restorePreEdit`), clear `sm-running`, report it, and say "That took too long · nothing changed".
   - **Tiles only when visible.** fx tiles are generated only while on screen (an IntersectionObserver on `.sm-fx-run`), and the ticker stops when the picker closes.
3. If cause 3 is measured: Simple's preview draws adjustment layers at the playback tier's reduced size. Coordinate this with G6.

**Tests:**
- **380:** within 1 s of the tile tap, there is no `sm-running` and `S.running` is false; 600 ms of play advances `FM.time` by ≥ 0.4 s; a click selects a clip.
- **Watchdog:** a plan that never settles, on a fake clock, is cleared after 8 s with the scene unchanged and one line said. **Mutation:** remove the watchdog's clear, and it goes red.
- The measured cause gets its own catching test (prove.sh wants one per queue item).

**No pick needed.** **Size:** M. **Depends on:** S10, G6.
