The new button’s icon — pick a letter

It opens the pop-up holding playback speed, loop, the magnet and the export marks. Shown big, then at the size it actually ships at, sitting next to its neighbours in the row.

A Sliders — recommended
Reads as “settings for this row” at a glance, and nothing else in the app uses it. Shipped in the picture I sent.
B A timeline with its marks
Says WHICH options — the ones about the timeline. Busier at 24px, and it can read as another playhead.
C Speed dial
Points straight at the speed stepper, which is the control you will reach for most. Says nothing about the magnet or the marks.
D The magnet
Borrows the icon of a control that is already inside the pop-up, so the button and one of its contents look the same. Cheap to recognise, easy to confuse.

Greyed icons either side are its real neighbours in the row. Say a letter and it changes; say nothing and A stays.
