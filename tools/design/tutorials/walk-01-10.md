# T6: tutorials 01 to 10 walked in the real app at 380 px (main v17.25)

Base `origin/main` 2e3fd7a9 (v17.25). Report only: no tutorial or app file was changed. Each tutorial was walked on a **fresh Chromium profile at 380x760 with mobile and touch emulation on** (headless Chromium 1194, software GL), by a script that does what the step says and saves a screenshot after it (`shots/`, JPEG, one or two per step). The table has one row per step of the tutorial, in the tutorial's own words.

**What was real, and what was not.**
- **Real touch** (`Input.dispatchTouchEvent`): every tap, every drag, every press-and-hold (0.6 to 0.7 s), the two-finger pinch in 03, and the grip-hold reorder in 07.
- **Not drivable in a headless browser, so done another way:** the OS file picker (the picker was intercepted and handed a file), the native colour picker (`<input type=color>`, set by script; the hex box beside it was typed into), the native `<select>` menus in the Export box (set by script), and **scrolling by touch: native touch scrolling does not move even a plain test div here, so panels were scrolled with the mouse wheel**. Where a step needs a scroll the tutorial never mentions, the row says so.
- **Media:** a 6 s VP8 `.webm` stood in for a phone video, a generated `.wav` stood in for a song. This container has **no H.264 or AAC**, so MP4 export cannot run: in tutorial 01 the tap on **Export MP4** froze the tab (CDP timed out), so steps 11 and 12 of 01 and step 9 of 09 are marked **not walked**. Whether the MP4 export freezes on a phone is not known. GIF export did run to the end (Done, saved to your Downloads, about 80 s).
- **Not tested at all:** sound (no audio device), the "On a computer" paragraphs (this is the 380 px pass), and the Tip / "If it doesn't work" lines unless a step row says otherwise.

## Summary
- **Wrong or missing as written (each has a replacement in its row):** 02 step 6 (the handle can be off the screen after a split), 03 steps 6, 7, 8 and 9 (the Effects panel is short and scrolls, so Threshold softness, Glow past the edges and the Shadow colour are all below the fold, and opening one row closes the other), 06 step 4 ("a sound-wave bar": it is an orange bar with the song's name) and step 8 (the timeline glides after a swipe, so the song is cut a fraction late unless the reader is careful), 08 steps 7 and 8 (the two tick boxes are below the fold).
- **Not walked here:** 01 steps 11 and 12, 09 step 9 (MP4 export).
- **Everything else worked as written**, including the exact words in the lists and buttons (Add menu tabs, the six export formats, the five button labels, the three trim buttons, the delete box wording).
