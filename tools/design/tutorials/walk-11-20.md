# T7: tutorials 11 to 20 walked in the real app at 380 px (main v17.25)

Base `origin/main` 2e3fd7a9 (v17.25). Report only: no tutorial or app file was changed. Same method as `walk-01-10.md` (T6): fresh Chromium profile at 380x760, mobile and touch emulation, real touch events for every tap, drag and hold, a screenshot after each step (`shots/`, JPEG). Same limits: the file picker was intercepted and handed a file, native colour pickers and selects were set by script, panels were scrolled with the mouse wheel because native touch scrolling does not move anything in this headless browser, a 6 s VP8 `.webm` stood in for a phone video, and there is no sound, no H.264 and no second device or network. The "On a computer" lines were not tested.

## Summary
- **Does not work as written (1):** 13 step 5. The eraser does **not** remove a whole stroke; it rubs out only what the finger passes over, so a short swipe at the end of a stroke thins it and a swipe across a stroke cuts it in two. Replacement is in the row.
- **Works, but the tutorial needs a line (2):** 17 step 7 (stopping asks a second question, "Stop sharing?", and you tap **Stop sharing** again), and 18 step 1 (on a fresh install the Templates tab is empty and says "No templates yet", so the reader has to save one first, which is step 5).
- **Not walked (2 steps):** 17 steps 5 and 6 need a second device on a real network.
- **Everything else worked as written:** 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 19 and 20 had no wrong step. Small things seen and left alone because the words are still true: 12 (adding a cue at the playhead can overlap the next cue, the sheet scrolls), 15 (the camera panel tabs are icons with tooltips), 17 (no separate Share button on this screen), 19 (if the add sheet is still open, the first back-arrow tap only closes it), 20 (the Tip's "two small buttons next to" the loop button are Copy beside it and Paste below it).
