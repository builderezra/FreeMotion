# AU27: Phase 2's 2.1 and 2.2 before they ship (`980-p22-r3`, on a copy)

Copy of `origin/980-p22-r3` (a51b5e1d, 7 Oct; the newest 2.x tip on the remote: `980-p21-r3` is its parent, and the older `980-p22-trayb2` / `-fix` branches are 6 Oct). 8 commits past main 470ee20e; its own commit says "NOT YET RUN". **Measured** = I ran it here (headless Chromium on Linux, 1280 and 380), **Read** = read the code, **Guess** = not checked. I touched none of the builder's branches.

## Result: no defect found that is red on the copy. I am saying so plainly rather than inventing one.
What I tried, each one a way a Simple edit could lose work or leave a save Full cannot open. All held:

1. **Undo across a run of commands (Measured, `AU27-1`).** The builder's T2 proves ONE command undoes byte for byte. I ran 8 commands in a row (delete, both trims, split, close a gap, duplicate) over 6 seeded random main tracks, then walked the whole stack back with `undo` checking every document, then forward with `redo`, and did it twice: once in Simple and once after switching to Full for the walk (undo across editors). Green at 1280 and 380.
2. **A Simple save Full cannot open (Measured, `AU27-2`).** After each of ten commands, every layer's `sm` and the project's `sm` are a fixed point of the loader's own sanitiser (`sanitizeSmLayer` / `sanitizeSmProject`). If the loader changed what Simple just wrote (for example `sm.main` deleting `stay`, `tail` and `tailEnd`), that edit would be lost on the next open. Green at both widths; **mutation CAUGHT**: making the loader also strip `sm.stay` turns AU27-1, 2 and 3 red.
3. **The 2.2 plans in sequence (Measured, `AU27-3`).** `planReorder`, `planLift`, `planIntoRow`, `planStay`, `planZ` and `planCloseAll` mixed with split, duplicate and trim over 8 seeds: 62 commands were accepted across all nine kinds in the first run (reorder 6, lift 13, into row 8, stay 6, z 1, close all 4, split 9, duplicate 7, trim 8; `z` is thin), before the no-step filter; the final run only counts those that wrote a step. Each undoes to the document before it and redoes to the one after; none changed the document without an undo step; the loader leaves each write alone. Green at both widths; **mutation CAUGHT**: making Duplicate skip its history commit turns AU27-1 and AU27-3 red.
   - **A false alarm worth recording:** the first version of AU27-3 was red ("undo of a stay step did not restore"), and that was my harness, not the app. A command that changes nothing (a Stay on a clip already staying) legally leaves no undo step, and I had logged a step for it. The test now counts history steps and fails only when a command changes the document with NO step.
4. **Double taps and the queue (Measured, `audit-simple-2x-scripts/au27_dbl.js`, `au27_q.js`).** Two Splits at one time: the second is refused with "Too close to the edge of the clip. Trim instead?" and the document is right. Two Deletes of one clip: the second answers "That clip was just deleted", nothing double-removed. Six Duplicates in one breath: one runs, four queue, the sixth says "One moment, still finishing the last edit." and writes nothing (7 layers: 2 + 5). By design, and the line shows.
5. **Phone layout at 380 (Measured, `au27_tray.js`).** The tray is one scrolling row on a phone (`#sm-tray` `overflow-x: auto`, hidden scrollbar; More and Delete are pinned to the end on purpose, review finding 22). For a video clip the eight tools are 507 px wide in a 380 px row, so **Crop sits past the right edge** until the row is swiped, with no visible hint that it scrolls. That is the designed behaviour, not a bug; whether a phone tray with a hidden scrollbar and no fade is discoverable is a design call, noted for the builder. No tool is under the 44 px target (all 50 px tall, 54 to 83 wide) and the page does not overflow.
6. **The refusals in §6.1.** Read: `busyReason` covers export, recording, a running spine edit or a read in flight, and a live drag (the dead `canvasGestureLive` seam is AU24's, and it is defined on the copy). I did not re-test them here.
