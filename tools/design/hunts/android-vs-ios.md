## The five worth acting on first (all need an Android device to confirm)
1. **Back button.** Nothing in the app owns history, so on Android Back from a sheet or card leaves the editor (Guess about the exact outcome; the code side is Read). The smallest fix is one `pushState` when any sheet, card or the editor opens and a `popstate` that closes the top-most thing first; the app already has a single place that knows what is open (the back-arrow ladder at js/mobile.js:343 and app.js:6905). Test: with the editor open, `history.back()` closes the open sheet and does not change the page.
2. **AAC on Android.** Open the app on a real Android phone, export a project with a song, read the card's meta line and the "AAC encode" line of the export report (exporter.js:1586). If it says AudioEncoder is missing or unsupported, Android exports are silent on that device and the plan for #215/#604/#677 must include a non-AAC route (Opus in MP4 is not widely playable; the realistic fix is an AAC encoder in JS or WASM).
3. **Manifest.** `"orientation": "portrait"` (manifest.json:8) removes landscape for installed Android users; decide whether that is wanted. Split the icon `purpose` into one `any` and one `maskable` image.
4. **HEIC.** Decide whether a HEIC picked on Android should be converted (a canvas cannot decode it there either) or refused with a clear sentence; today it is the generic "could not open" report.
5. **Persistence.** Show the result of `persist()` (the #1018 item) and, for Android, suggest installing the app.

## What I checked and found already handled
Haptics, the Android long-press `contextmenu`, wake lock, QR scanning, audio contexts, voice-recording containers, picker lists, canvas filter fallbacks. The share path falls back to a plain download.
