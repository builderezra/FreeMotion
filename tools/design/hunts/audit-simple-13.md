## One finding, red on the copy, green with a 6-line fix at 1280 and 380

**AU24-1 (Measured, §6.1): the editor switch does not refuse while a finger is dragging a layer on the canvas.** DESIGN.md §6.1 lists the refusals "an export, a recording, a Simple command in flight, **a timeline or canvas drag live** (shake only)". `busyReason()` in `editor-mode.js` asks `FM.timeline.gestureLive()` (which exists, with a stale-gesture recovery) **and `FM.canvasGestureLive`, which nothing in the app defines** (grep over `js/`, `tests/`: the only mention is the call, and BUILD-PLAN.md quoting it). So the guard's second half has never fired. Measured: with a shape selected, a pointer press on `#preview` and two moves (the layer's x went 270 to 397, the drag live), `FM.editor.request('simple')` **switched to Simple mid-drag** (`gestureLive: false`, `canvasGestureLive: "undefined"`). Reachable on a phone with two fingers (one drags, one taps the cog); the switch rebuilds the timeline and re-lays the screen under the dragging finger, the thing §6.1's rule exists to prevent.
- **Fix:** `canvas-edit.js` defines `FM.canvasGestureLive = () => !!(drag || vpPinch)` (a read-only addition; `canvas-edit.js?v=121` to `122`). Test `AU24-1`: red on the copy, green with it at 1280 and 380; mutation (drop the definition) CAUGHT by the same test.
- **Caveats for the builder:** (1) the pinch half (`vpPinch`) is in the fix but the test drives only a one-pointer drag. (2) `FM.timeline.gestureLive()` recovers a *stuck* gesture (lost pointer, queue 541) before refusing; my seam does not, so a canvas `drag` that never sees its pointerup would refuse every switch with a shake and no line until the next canvas press. `FM._resetVpPointers` already exists as the way to clear it; wiring the same recovery in is the safe next step. (3) The change is in a **Full** file; the FU lock (§0.4.5) will need to accept a read-only addition (it changes no behaviour of Full).
