# AU15 the audio code: preview equals export, mute and solo, nothing left running after a stop

Branch `hunt/audit-audio`, against main fba44ca8 (v17.32). Labels: Measured / Read / Guess. Scripts and raw numbers in `audit-audio-scripts/`. Everything ran in headless Chromium on Linux with autoplay allowed (`--autoplay-policy=no-user-gesture-required`): **no real speakers, no iPhone, no Safari.** WAV and WebM-free (audio-only clips made in the page).

**Result: no bug found, no code changed.** One low-value observation at the end. Said plainly, because a clean result from 800 lines of reading plus nine probes is a statement about those probes, not about all audio code.

## What I read (Read)
`js/audio-fx-live.js` (the first 330 lines: routing, boost and level stages, the limiter), `js/audio-play.js` (the reversed voice, its buffer builder and gain schedule), the mixer `buildAudioMix` in `js/exporter.js` (477 to 800), the playback tick's audio half in `js/app.js` (already read in AU13), `FM.soloSilenced` (`scene.js`), and `js/audio-fx-browser.js` for timers and teardown. **Not read:** the 27 audio effect implementations in `js/audio-fx.js` (1669 lines) beyond their registry and `buildAudioFxChain`, `audio-react.js`, `audio-tools.js`, `audio-health.js`, and `encodeM4A`.

## Probes and what they showed
1. **The level the export mixes equals the level the preview plays (Measured).** `au15_mix.js` runs the real `FM.exporter.buildAudioMix` on a 0.4-amplitude tone and compares the rendered peak at sample moments with `layerVolume x fadeMul x 0.4` (the preview's formula): static 0.5, boost 1.5 (through the limiter), fade in and out, keyframed volume, keyframed volume plus fade, muted, hidden (returns no mix, as it should), a 2 to 4 s export range, a fade that straddles the range, a clip that starts at 1.5 s, trimStart, speed 2, volume keyframed to 0. **13 of 13 agree** (the one pair that looked 9 % apart, at 0.25 s of the keyframed-plus-fade case, is my probe taking the peak over a 50 ms window of a rising ramp; the point value matches).
2. **The preview's own element levels follow the same formula (Measured, `au15_prev.js`).** While playing: `el.volume` read every 250 ms equals the formula to three decimals for a 0.2 to 1 keyframed ramp and a 2 s fade-in at 0.6; a muted clip reads `muted = true` at every sample.
3. **Mute, solo, hidden in the preview (Measured, AU13 `play.js` and `au15_rev.js`).** Soloing one clip pauses and mutes the other and leaves the soloed one sounding; muted and volume 0 reach `el.muted`; for a REVERSED clip (a Web Audio voice): hidden, inside a hidden group, and another layer soloed each give 0 running voices; plain, muted, volume 0, itself soloed, keyframed to 0 and fade-in give 1 voice (the voice exists with its gain at 0 for muted and zero volume: Read in `scheduleGain`). The exporter's gate (`hidden || groupHidden || soloActive && !solo`) is the same three rules (Read), and a solo on a group solos its descendants in both (`app.js` 6021 to 6024).
4. **Reversed audio, preview against export (Measured, `au15_revcmp2.js`).** The preview's synthesized reversed buffer (`m._revBuf`) and the exported mix of the same clip, compared in time after resampling both to a common grid, on a rising chirp: plain, trimStart 1.5, speed 1.5, a 3 s clip of 8 s of audio, a 6 s clip over 4 s of audio, and a speed ramp 1 to 2. **Relative error 0.0000 and best alignment 0.00 ms in all six.** (The container's AudioContext runs at 44.1 kHz and the export at 48 kHz; the comparison is by time, not by sample index.)
5. **Audio effects, live against export (Measured, `au15_fx.js`).** For each of the 27 audio effects, the same chain is rendered offline two ways: the export's (`chain.schedule(0, T)`) and the preview's (`applyAt` every 1/60 s, using `OfflineAudioContext.suspend`), on a 2.5 s tone-plus-noise source. **With default (static) parameters: 27 of 27 are identical (relative difference 0.0000).** With the first keyframable parameter swept from its minimum to its maximum over the clip: 12 agree within 2.5 % (bassTreble, eq3, telephone, width, pan, gain, compressor, limiter, distortion, lofi, vocalremove, bitcrush 5 %); the others differ by 6 % to 140 % (lowpass 7 %, notch 6 %, highpass 15 %, reverb 14 %, autopan 17 %, tremolo 20 %, bandpass 22 %, phaser 30 %, chorus 36 %, pingpong 42 %, flanger 55 %, delay 69 %, vibrato 72 %, pitch 112 %, ringmod 140 %). **I did NOT call these bugs:** they are all parameters that act through PHASE or DELAY (an oscillator's rate, a delay time, a filter sweep). Applying a frequency 60 times a second against ramping it continuously integrates to a different phase after a large sweep (about half a frame of the sweep range per step, several full cycles over a 2000 Hz ringmod sweep), and a root-mean-square difference of two waveforms that differ only in phase reads as large while sounding the same. A fair test needs a spectral or envelope comparison, which I did not write, and an ear. **Left as a measured gap, not a finding.**
6. **Leaks after a stop (Measured, `au15_leak.js`, `au15_rev.js`, `au15_proj.js`).** Every running `AudioBufferSourceNode`, `OscillatorNode` and `ConstantSourceNode` was counted by wrapping `start()` and listening for `ended`. A clip with four LFO effects (tremolo, chorus, autopan, phaser): 6 sources while playing. After Pause: 6. After a second play and pause: **6 (it does not grow)**. After the effects are disabled, removed from the layer, the layer is deleted, or another project is opened: **0**. A reversed voice: 1 while playing and 0 after Pause. Audition: 1 while auditioning, 1 after it ends (the idle chain of a layer that still has the effect).

## The one observation (LOW, not fixed)
A layer that has audio effects keeps its chain, and with it its LFO sources, running while the project is **paused** (6 sources for four effects, until the effects are removed or the layer or project goes). Cause (Read, `audio-fx-live.js`): the chain is deliberately kept because a `MediaElementSource` can be made once per element and never undone, and a paused element feeds it silence. The cost is idle audio-graph work, and I did not measure it or find a place it matters (on a phone, battery, Guess). A fix would stop and rebuild the LFOs around play and pause; I judge the risk of that (a rebuilt chain is where a clip goes permanently silent, per the file's own comments) larger than the gain.

## Not covered
