#927 · Notes and Shortcuts: grab the corner to make them BIG

Built and working on branch fm-design-927. Every picture below is the real app, not a drawing.

Two things for you to pick: the look of the corner (1) and how big BIG is on PC (2). The rest shows how it moves.

How it was checked: real mouse and touch input in the browser, on PC and phone sizes. Not yet tried on your iPhone.

1The grab corner

It sits on the corner facing into the screen: top-left on PC (the panel hangs above its button at the bottom right), top-right on the phone (clear of Done, Tutorials and Close). On PC the cursor turns into ↖↘ over it. On the phone it is a 48px target, and a plain tap on it works too.

A · Corner arc RECOMMENDED

A rounded bracket that follows the panel’s own corner, like the handle on iPad windows. Reads as “grab here” without words, and grows a little under the cursor.

B · Grip lines

Three diagonal ridges, the mark on the corner of desktop windows and text boxes. Familiar on PC, quieter on the phone.

C · Expand button

A round button with arrows out (arrows in when it is big). The most obvious on a phone, but it reads as a button to tap more than a corner to drag.

2How big is BIG (on PC)

Always in the middle of the screen, and the same size for both panels. Shown with a project that has 14 notes. On the phone BIG is always the whole screen, whichever you pick here.

Roomy · up to 640 × 580

Keeps most of the editor showing, but it is barely taller than small: 6 notes in view, against 5 when small.

Focus · up to 880 × 820 RECOMMENDED

Covers most of the screen and keeps a comfortable line length: 9 notes in view. All 30 shortcuts fit in two columns with no scrolling.

Most of the screen · 32px margins

Nearly full screen, 10 notes in view, but on a wide monitor each note becomes one very long line.

Going BIG You said: “it expands into a bigger view where it basically covers up more of the screen and is in the center”

Drag the corner out. The panel leans after you a little, and once you are far enough a dashed outline shows the BIG size. Let go there and it grows into the middle; let go short of it and it springs back. There are only two sizes.

Back to small You said: “if you grab the edges again and drag it back in again it’ll go back to smaller”

When it is BIG, every edge and every corner works on PC, including the bottom-right where your hand goes first, each with its own resize cursor. Push in, a dashed outline shows where small will land, let go, and it goes back above its button.

Closing while BIG You said: “it kind of like shrinks down onto itself before closing so it’s like shrinking and then folding into the button”

It shrinks onto itself with your notes still on it, then tips back and goes into the button it came from. The button gives a small bump as it takes it. The whole close takes about half a second. Closing while small is instant, as it is today.

How it behaves

  • Two sizes only. Pull out past the outline and it goes BIG in the middle. Stop short and it springs back.
  • Back in from any side on PC when it is BIG: every edge and corner, each with its resize cursor. When small, the corner and the two edges beside it.
  • A tap on the corner switches it too, which is easier on the phone. On PC, Enter or Space on the corner also works.
  • Remembered for Notes and Shortcuts separately, every time you open them.
  • Reduce Motion on: the same two sizes, no animation, and it closes instantly.
