#927 · Notes and Shortcuts: grab the corner to make them BIG Built and working on branch fm-design-927 — tested with a real mouse and a real finger. Every picture is the real app. Two things for you to pick.

1The grab corner

Sits on the corner facing into the screen: top-left on PC (the panel hangs above its button, bottom-right), top-right on the phone (clear of Done, Tutorials and Close). Cursor becomes ↖↘ over it on PC. 48px to grab on the phone.

A · Corner arc RECOMMENDED

A rounded bracket that follows the panel’s own corner — the handle iPad windows use. Reads as “grab here” without a word; grows a little under the cursor.

B · Grip lines

Three diagonal ridges — the mark on the corner of desktop windows and text boxes. Familiar on PC; quieter on the phone.

C · Expand button

A round button with arrows out (turns to arrows in when big). Says what it does, most obvious on a phone — but reads as a button to tap more than a corner to drag.

2How big is BIG (on PC)

Always centred, same size for both panels. On the phone BIG is simply the whole screen, whichever you pick.

Roomy · up to 640 × 580

A clearly bigger panel that still leaves most of the editor showing. Shortcuts stay one column.

Focus · up to 880 × 820 RECOMMENDED

Covers most of the screen but keeps a comfortable line length for reading notes. Shortcuts go two columns, so nearly all of them fit without scrolling.

Most of the screen · 32px margins

Nearly full screen. On a wide monitor the note lines get very long.

Going BIG — the animation

Drag the corner out. It leans after you a little; past the point a dashed outline shows the BIG size. Let go and it grows into the middle. Let go short of it and it springs back. Only two sizes.

Closing while BIG — shrink, then fold into the button

It shrinks down onto itself first, then tips back and folds into the button it came from, which gives a small bump as it takes it. (Closing while small is instant, as today.)

How it behaves

  • Two sizes only. Pull the corner — or the edge next to it — out past the outline: BIG, centred. Short of it: springs back.
  • Drag it back in (corner or edge): small again, back above its button.
  • A tap on the corner switches too — easier on the phone.
  • Remembered, for Notes and Shortcuts separately, every time you open them.
  • Reduce Motion on: same two sizes, no animation, closes instantly.
Recommended: A · Corner arc + Focus size.
