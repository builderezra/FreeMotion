Ten new filters, judged on your own photographs

The four thin sections of the Filters tab were Punchy (4), Retro (5), Light / Glow (5) and Cinematic (7). These ten fill them out. Every row below is rendered by the app itself, on the same fx‑art photograph its thumbnail will use, beside every filter that section already has, so a new one is judged against its neighbours and not on its own.

Punchy

Had Poppy, Candy, Sunbaked, Ultraviolet.

Tropic Punchy

Greens and blues pushed hard, warm skin kept. Holiday water and foliage.

Changed after round one: the first draft warmed the whole frame and came out as a second Sunbaked. Now it cools and greens instead, so the greens and blues are what move.

saturate 1.22 · contrast 1.06 · vibrance 2.4 (skin 45) · temperature −8 / tint −14 · highlights −12 / shadows +14

Popsicle Punchy

Bright, cool and pink in the highlights. A sweet‑shop palette rather than a sunny one.

saturate 1.3 · brightness 1.06 · colour balance R+12 G−8 B+20 · tint #ff66cc 0.22 on highlights

Hi‑Vis Punchy

Every colour turned up as far as it goes before it breaks, with the blacks set hard.

contrast 1.1 · saturate 1.15 · vibrance 3.2 (skin 20) · levels 10 … 240

Retro / Analogue

Had VHS Tape, CRT Monitor, Super 8, Old Film, Copperplate.

Polaroid Retro

Lifted blacks, a warm‑green cast and soft corners. The instant print, a few years on.

brightness 1.03 · contrast 0.94 · faded 0.7 (lift 30, desat 20) · temperature +22 / tint +6 · colour balance G+6 B−14 · vignette 0.45 · grain 18

Kodachrome Retro

Deep reds, clean blues and a little extra contrast. The slide film everyone remembers.

Changed after round one: too timid to register at a glance. Reds, contrast and the black point all came up a step; it is still a grade, not a look.

saturate 1.28 · contrast 1.14 · colour balance R+20 G−4 B−12 · temperature +14 · levels 12 … 244, gamma 0.94 · grain 12

Technicolor Retro

Three‑strip colour, saturated to the point of painted, reds and cyans leading.

saturate 1.45 · contrast 1.15 · vibrance 1.8 · colour balance R+10 G−6 B+6 · highlights −8 / shadows +6

Light / Glow

Had Dreamy Bloom, Golden Hour, Light Leak, Neon Night, Ember.

Halo Glow

The red‑orange bleed film puts around bright lights, plus a soft wash. Glow that reads as film rather than video.

Changed after round one: the first two drafts leaned on a warm shift and read as Golden Hour. The warmth is gone and the bleed itself is pushed near the top of its range, short of the ends; it now shows on a blown sunset. It is the subtlest of the ten and the first to drop if you want fewer.

halation 1.85 (threshold 0.42, core 0.3, spread 13) · soft glow 0.42 (radius 160, threshold 40)

Moonbeam Glow

Cool, quiet and softly blooming. Moonlight rather than lamplight.

brightness 1.02 · saturate 0.85 · temperature −28 / tint +4 · light glow 0.5 (radius 22, threshold 55) · vignette 0.3

Cinematic

Had Teal & Orange, Bleach Bypass, Cross Process, Faded Film, Matte, Moonlight, Low Key.

Arctic Cinematic

Cold and pale with the blacks lifted. Snow light, thin air.

contrast 1.04 · saturate 0.9 · temperature −34 / tint +6 · faded 0.35 (lift 14) · levels 0 … 240

Desert Cinematic

Hot yellow light against teal shadows, highlights held back, a touch of dust.

saturate 1.05 · contrast 1.08 · temperature +30 / tint −8 · teal & orange 0.4 (pivot 55) · highlights −20 / shadows +10 · grain 10

What to reply

Nothing, if all ten are fine. They ship as they are here, each one an ordinary filter you can open, change or strip the strength off.

Otherwise name any to drop ("drop Halo"), or say what a look should do differently ("Polaroid warmer"). Every value sits inside its slider's range and away from the ends, so any of them can be pushed further on request.
